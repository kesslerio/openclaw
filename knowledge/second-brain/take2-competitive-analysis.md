## 🎯 Executive Summary

Take2 just raised **$14M Series A** (Feb 11, 2026) for AI-powered healthcare recruiting. While they focus on **hiring/HR**, Copper AI focuses on **patient care operations**. Different verticals, but shared learnings about how to position and sell voice AI in healthcare.

**Key Takeaway:** Their success validates the healthcare voice AI market and provides a blueprint for Copper AI's positioning and sales strategy.

## Product: AI Interviewer

### What It Does

- **24/7 phone interviews** with healthcare job candidates
- **Evaluates candidates** using healthcare-specific hiring data
- **Records calls** and transcribes
- **Syncs results** directly into ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)
- **Predictive insights** for hiring quality and retention
- **No human-in-the-loop** — fully autonomous

### Key Features

1. Voice-first (phone interviews)
2. Healthcare-trained models (credentialing, regulatory)
3. ATS integrations
4. Retention prediction
5. End-to-end automation (sourcing → onboarding)

### Expansion Roadmap (Post-Series A)

- Sourcing automation
- Screening automation
- Credential verification
- Scheduling
- Employee onboarding
- Full "agent network" for recruiting

## Market Context

### Healthcare Hiring Pain Points (from Take2's pitch)

- **1 in 3 new US jobs** are in healthcare
- **50%+ turnover** rates common
- **Vacancy costs** among highest of any industry
- **Manual workflows** dominate recruiting
- **CHRO quote:** "Faster recruiting, more economical, scalable, better candidate experience"

### Customer Profile

- **Target:** Large health systems, DSOs (Dental Service Organizations)
- **Traction:** "Top 10 Health Systems" as customers
- **Growth:** "Quadrupled customer base in 6 months"

## 🔥 What Copper AI Can Learn

### 1. Positioning That Worked

**Take2's Message:**

> "AI agents that actually do the work — not just assist"

**Apply to Copper AI:**

> "AI agents that make the calls, not just suggest them"
> "Voice AI that handles patient outreach — not just reminders"

### 2. Pain Point Framing

**Take2:**

- Healthcare hiring = "breaking point"
- 50% turnover = massive cost
- Manual workflows = bottleneck

**Copper AI equivalent:**

- Caregiver no-shows = breaking point
- 15-30% no-show rate = massive cost
- Manual reminder calls = bottleneck

### 3. Social Proof Strategy

**Take2:**

- Specific quote from CHRO at Mercy Health
- Named "Top 10 Health Systems" (without naming)
- "Quadrupled customer base" growth metric

**Copper AI should:**

- Get one strong testimonial from pilot agency
- Reference "leading home health agencies" once deployed
- Track and share growth metrics

### 4. Funding Narrative

**Take2's story to investors:**

- Healthcare = massive market (1 in 3 jobs)
- Existing solutions = manual, broken
- AI agents = automation that "actually works"
- Traction = proof of product-market fit

**Copper AI's story:**

- Home health = $20B+ market
- No-shows = 15-30% (massive cost)
- Voice AI = proven 30-50% reduction in no-shows
- ClearCare integration = 4,500 agency TAM
