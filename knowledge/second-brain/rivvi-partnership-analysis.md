## Platform Capabilities

### Four Core Capabilities

1. **Aegis (Voice AI)**
   - Natural patient conversations (not robocalls)
   - Creates tasks, triggers notifications
   - Orchestrates workflows across care teams
   - Complete automation from conversation to closed care gap

2. **Data Intelligence**
   - Reconciles fragmented healthcare data
   - Unified patient records from scattered sources
   - Prioritized worklists

3. **Governance**
   - Clinical policy enforcement at infrastructure level
   - Contact rules, escalation paths, compliance boundaries
   - Every conversation auditable and traceable

4. **Safety**
   - Patient harm prevention built into every conversation
   - Crisis detection in seconds
   - Medical advice guardrails
   - Seamless human escalation

## Why This Matters for Copper AI

### Current Stack (Retell + ElevenLabs)

- Retell: $0.07-0.08/min (voice) + $0.003-0.08/min (LLM) + $0.015/min (telephony)
- **Total: ~$0.13-0.31/min**
- General-purpose, not healthcare-native
- We handle compliance ourselves

### Rivvi Advantages

1. **Healthcare-Native**: Built for healthcare from day one, not adapted
2. **Safety Built-In**: Crisis detection, medical guardrails (we'd need to build)
3. **Governance Layer**: Compliance enforcement at infrastructure level
4. **Data Intelligence**: Can reconcile fragmented patient data
5. **Workflow Automation**: Beyond just calls - full care gap closure
6. **Bootstrapped**: Aligned incentives, no VC pressure to raise prices

### Potential Partnership Models

**Option A: Infrastructure Partner**

- Use Rivvi as underlying voice infrastructure
- Copper AI = home health vertical specialization layer
- Rivvi handles voice/safety/governance
- We handle home health workflows (EVV, scheduling, caregiver matching)

**Option B: Reseller/White-Label**

- Rebrand Rivvi for home health market
- Add our home health-specific features on top
- Leverage their 40:1 capacity multiplier

**Option C: Co-Development**

- Build home health-specific Aegis agents together
- Share learnings on home health market
- Potential for joint go-to-market
