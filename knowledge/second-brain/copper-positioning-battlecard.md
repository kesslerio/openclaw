## 🥊 Head-to-Head Battles

### vs **Vapi AI**

**When to expect this competitor:** Developer-savvy prospects, those who think they need "maximum flexibility"

**Their pitch:**

- "Build exactly what you want"
- "Bring your own stack"
- "Sub-500ms latency"
- "100+ languages"

**Our counter:**

> "Vapi is an incredible platform... **if you have a full-time developer**. Their $0.05/min looks great until you realize you need 4-6 different providers — each with their own bill. By the time you add Deepgram ($0.01/min), GPT-4 ($0.10-0.20/min), ElevenLabs ($0.07/min), and Twilio ($0.01/min), you're at **$0.30+ per minute**. Plus 40-80 hours/month maintaining it.
>
> Copper gives you the same quality voice AI, but we handle ALL the integration for you. **One bill. Half the cost. Zero code.** And we're built specifically for home health — EVV sync, compliance, caregiver scheduling — not generic voice."

**Win conditions:**

- ✅ Prospect has NO in-house developers
- ✅ Prospect wants predictable monthly costs
- ✅ Prospect needs vertical features (EVV, compliance)
- ❌ Lose if: They have a dev team and want maximum control

## 🎯 Key Objection Handlers

### "Why not just use [Competitor X]?"

> "Great question. [Competitor] is a solid platform, but it's built for **developers/sales teams/generic businesses**. Every feature we build asks: 'Does this help a home health agency care for patients better?' If not, we don't build it. You're not paying for features you'll never use."

### "Your pricing seems too good to be true — what's the catch?"

> "No catch. We just made smarter choices in our tech stack. Most competitors use ElevenLabs TTS at $0.30/1K characters. We use fish.audio at $0.01/1K — same quality, 30x cheaper. Those savings go to you, not our margins."

### "We need [Feature X] that [Competitor] has"

> "Let's talk about that. If it's critical for home health operations, we probably have it or can build it fast — we're laser-focused on this vertical. If it's a generic feature, ask yourself: do you really need it, or is it just nice to have?"

### "We're worried about switching costs"

> "Switching FROM generic platforms to Copper typically takes **3-5 days**, not weeks. And we include migration support. The real switching cost is staying on an expensive, complex platform that wasn't built for you."

## 📊 When We Win vs When We Lose

### ✅ We Win When:

- Prospect is a **home health agency** (not generic healthcare)
- They have **no in-house developers**
- They want **predictable monthly costs**
- They value **vertical-specific features**
- They're **cost-conscious** but quality-minded
- They handle **200-5,000 calls/month** (our sweet spot)

### ❌ We Lose When:

- Prospect has a **full-time dev team** and wants maximum control → Vapi/Retell
