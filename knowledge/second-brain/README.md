## 🎯 The Concept (1-Minute Pitch)

**You have:** 2,500+ meeting transcripts trapped in Plaud.AI  
**You want:** An AI that remembers everything and answers "What did I promise Mark?"

**We'll build:**

1. **Scraper** - Liberate your data (Week 1-2)
2. **Vector DB** - Store semantic meanings (Week 2-3)
3. **Recency Engine** - Favor recent memories over old ones ⭐ (Week 3)
4. **Journal Generator** - Auto-create daily summaries with diagrams (Week 3-4)
5. **Chat Interface** - Ask questions, get answers with sources (Week 4-5)

**Cost:** ~$5-25/month after $750 initial indexing  
**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks to MVP  
**Your time:** ~2-3 hours total (approve phases, test milestones)
