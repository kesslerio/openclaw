# Customer Success & Retention Playbook

_Created: Feb 2, 2026 | Nike's CS Strategy for Copper AI_

**Mission:** Turn customers into raving fans who never churn and refer others.

**Goal:** 95%+ retention rate (industry avg: 85-90%)

---

## Why Retention Matters More Than Acquisition

**The Math:**

- Acquiring a new customer: **$2,000-5,000** (marketing, sales, demos, onboarding)
- Retaining an existing customer: **$0-200** (just deliver value)
- **5% increase in retention = 25-95% increase in profits** (Bain & Company)

**For Copper AI:**

- If we lose 10% of customers annually: **$2,000 CAC × 30 churned customers = $60k wasted**
- If we lose 5% of customers annually: **$2,000 CAC × 15 churned customers = $30k wasted**
- **Difference: $30k saved per year** by improving retention 5%

**Conclusion:** Retention is more profitable than growth.

## The Customer Journey: Stage-by-Stage Retention

### Stage 1: Onboarding (Days 1-30)

**🎯 Goal:** Get agency to "aha moment" in first 7 days

**"Aha Moment" = First successful no-show prevention**

**Why This Matters:**

- Customers who see value in first 7 days: **95% retention**
- Customers who don't see value in first 30 days: **40% retention**

**The Clock is Ticking:** If no value by Day 30, they churn.

---

#### Day 1: Kickoff Call (30 minutes)

**Attendees:**

- Arvind (or Nike if voice AI can handle)
- Agency owner or operations manager
- Optional: Lead caregiver (to get buy-in)

**Agenda:**

**Minute 1-5: Set Expectations**

> "By the end of this week, you'll prevent your first no-show. By end of month, you'll see 30-50% reduction in no-shows. Our job is to make you wildly successful."

**Minute 5-10: Understand Their Pain**

> "What's your biggest challenge right now? No-shows? EVV compliance? Caregiver turnover?"

_(Take notes — customize onboarding based on their #1 pain)_

**Minute 10-20: Technical Setup**

- Add caregivers to Copper AI (names, phone numbers)
- Connect to ClearCare/Axxess (if applicable)
- Test call: "Let's call your phone right now and simulate a clock-in."

**Minute 20-25: Set Success Metrics**

> "Your current no-show rate is X%. Let's track this weekly. Goal: Reduce to Y% by end of month."

**Minute 25-30: Next Steps & Timeline**

- Day 2-3: Caregivers start using Copper AI
- Day 7: First check-in (15 min call)
- Day 14: Mid-month review (analyze metrics)
- Day 30: Success review (celebrate wins!)

**Deliverable:** Send follow-up email with:

- Meeting recap
- Login credentials
- Quick-start guide (1-page PDF)
- Calendar invite for Day 7 check-in

---

#### Day 2-3: Caregiver Training (Async)

**Don't rely on agencies to train caregivers — Copper AI does it.**

**How:**

1. Copper AI calls each caregiver:

   > "Hi Sarah, this is Copper AI. I'm the new voice assistant for [Agency Name]. From now on, you can call me to clock in/out, check your schedule, or confirm visits. Let's do a quick practice run. Ready?"

2. Walk through common scenarios:
   - "Say: Clock me in for Mrs. Johnson's visit."
   - "Say: What's my schedule today?"
   - "Say: I'm running 10 minutes late."

3. Confirm understanding:
   > "Great job! You're all set. Call me anytime at [number]. See you soon!"

**Why This Works:**

- Caregivers learn by doing (not reading a manual)
- Copper AI is patient (won't get frustrated if they mess up)
- Agencies don't have to train anyone (saves time)

**Success Metric:** 90% of caregivers complete training call within 48 hours

---

#### Day 7: First Check-In (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Catch issues early, celebrate early wins

**Agenda:**

**Minute 1-5: Celebrate Wins**

> "You prevented 3 no-shows this week! That's $450 saved. Amazing start."

_(Always lead with wins — positive reinforcement)_

**Minute 5-10: Address Issues**

> "Have any caregivers had trouble using Copper AI?"

Common issues:

- Caregiver forgot phone number (send SMS reminder)
- Caregiver doesn't speak English well (enable Spanish mode)
- Technical glitch (fix immediately)

**Minute 10-15: Set Next Week's Goal**

> "Let's aim for 5 no-shows prevented next week. I'll check in again on Day 14."

**Deliverable:** Send email recap with updated metrics

---

#### Day 14: Mid-Month Review (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Reinforce value, adjust strategy

**Review Metrics:**

- No-shows prevented (count + $$ saved)
- EVV compliance rate
- Caregiver adoption (% of caregivers using Copper AI)

**If Metrics Are Good:**

> "You're crushing it! 8 no-shows prevented = $1,200 saved in 2 weeks. Keep it up!"

**If Metrics Are Bad:**

> "I notice adoption is lower than expected. Let's troubleshoot. Which caregivers aren't using it? I can call them directly and help."

**Deliverable:** Midpoint report (simple 1-pager with metrics)

---

#### Day 30: Success Review & Lock-In (30 minutes)

**Goal:** Prove ROI, secure renewal commitment

**Agenda:**

**Minute 1-10: Present ROI**
Show month 1 results:

- No-shows prevented: [count]
- Money saved: $[amount]

- **Net savings: $[amount - 500]**
- **ROI: [X:1 ratio]**

**Minute 10-15: Gather Testimonial**

> "Can I ask you to record a short video testimonial? Just 60 seconds — what problem did Copper AI solve for you, and what results did you see?"

_(This becomes a case study for future sales)_

**Minute 15-25: Upsell / Lock-In**

> "Love that you're seeing results. Two options to lock in this value:
>
> 1. **Annual Plan:** Pay $5,000 upfront (save $1,000/year, 2 months free)
> 2. **Month-to-month:** Continue at $500/month
>
> Most agencies go annual because it's guaranteed savings. What makes sense for you?"

**Minute 25-30: Set Long-Term Goals**

> "What's your goal for Quarter 2? No-show rate below 3%? Let's track it together."

**Deliverable:**

- Month 1 success report (PDF with metrics, graphs, testimonial)
- Annual contract (if they sign)
- Q2 goal-setting email

**Success Metric:** 90% of customers who complete 30-day onboarding renew for Year 2

## Churn Prevention Playbook

### Early Warning Signs (Red Flags)

**Behavioral Signals:**

| Red Flag                             | What It Means                        | Action                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Declining call volume**            | Caregivers stopped using Copper AI   | Immediate call: "I notice usage dropped. What's going on?" |
| **No response to weekly emails**     | Disengaged, not seeing value         | Schedule check-in call                                     |
| **Skipped monthly check-in**         | Avoiding conversation (bad sign)     | Email: "Did I do something wrong?"                         |
| **Asked to pause subscription**      | Testing life without Copper AI       | Offer 50% discount for 1 month instead                     |
| **Asked about cancellation process** | 80% chance they churn within 30 days | Executive escalation (Arvind calls personally)             |

**Success Metric:** 90% of red flags resolved within 7 days

### Save-the-Customer Playbook

**When Customer Says: "We're canceling."**

**Step 1: Understand Why (Don't Argue)**

> "I'm sorry to hear that. Can you help me understand what happened? I want to learn from this."

**Step 2: Acknowledge Their Frustration**

> "That makes sense. I would feel the same way."

**Step 3: Offer Solution**
| Reason for Churn | Offer |
|------------------|-------|
| "Too expensive" | 50% discount for 3 months |
| "Not seeing results" | Free audit + optimization (identify what's broken) |
| "Too complicated" | Free re-onboarding (we'll fix it) |
| "We hired someone to handle this" | Copper AI complements humans (not replaces). Free trial extension to prove it. |

**Step 4: If They Still Want to Cancel**

> "I understand. Can I ask one favor? Give us 30 more days. If you still feel the same way, I'll cancel it myself. No questions asked."

**Step 5: Exit Interview**
If they still cancel:

> "Before you go, can I ask 3 quick questions? Your feedback will help us improve:
>
> 1. What did we do well?
> 2. What should we have done differently?
> 3. What would it take to win you back?"

**Deliverable:** Exit interview notes → Product roadmap updates

**Success Metric:** 50% of "canceling" customers stay (win-back rate)

**Net Retention Rate (NRR) Formula:**

```
NRR = (Starting ARR + Upsells - Churn) / Starting ARR
```

**Example:**

- Starting ARR: $100k
- Upsells: $20k (customers upgraded to annual plans)
- Churn: $5k (lost 1 customer)
- **NRR: ($100k + $20k - $5k) / $100k = 115%** 🎉

**Target: 110%+ NRR** (means you're growing revenue from existing customers faster than you're losing it)
