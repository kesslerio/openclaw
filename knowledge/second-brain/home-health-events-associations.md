## Conference Strategy for Copper AI

### Goal: Generate 20+ qualified leads per event

### Pre-Conference

- [ ] Register for event + booth
- [ ] Email attendee list (if available)
- [ ] Schedule meetings with target accounts
- [ ] Prepare:
  - Demo laptop/tablet
  - One-pagers (print 200+)
  - ROI calculators
  - Business cards
  - Swag (pens, notepads with branding)

### At Conference

- [ ] Man booth with demo ready
- [ ] Collect contact info (lead scanner or manual)
- [ ] Take photos for social media
- [ ] Attend relevant sessions
- [ ] Network at social events
- [ ] Identify competitors at other booths

### Post-Conference

- [ ] Send follow-up emails within 48 hours
- [ ] Connect on LinkedIn
- [ ] Schedule demos with hot leads
- [ ] Share photos/recap on social
- [ ] Track conversions in CRM
