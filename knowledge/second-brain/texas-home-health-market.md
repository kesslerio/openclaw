# Texas Home Health Market Research

_Last updated: Jan 31, 2026_

## Market Size & Opportunity

Texas is one of the largest home health markets in the US due to:

- Large elderly population
- Geographic spread (rural areas need home health)
- Medicaid expansion considerations
- Strong HHS regulatory framework

## Regulatory Context

Home and Community Support Services Agencies (HCSSA) in Texas are regulated by:

- Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC)
- Must maintain EVV compliance
- Subject to regular audits
- Medicare/Medicaid certification requirements

## Target Segments for Copper AI

### Tier 1: Medium Agencies (50-200 caregivers)

- Have scheduling complexity
- Not big enough for enterprise solutions
- Feel compliance burden most acutely
- Can afford ~$500-1000/month for automation

### Tier 2: Small Agencies (10-50 caregivers)

- Owner-operated, stretched thin
- Price-sensitive (~$100-300/month range)
- Need simplest possible setup
- Dialora's sweet spot - we need to compete here

### Tier 3: Large Agencies (200+ caregivers)

- Have existing systems
- Longer sales cycles
- Bigger contracts but harder to win
- May already have in-house solutions

## Key Pain Points (from research)

1. **EVV Compliance** - Texas requires it for Medicaid reimbursement
2. **Caregiver Turnover** - Industry avg 65%+ annually
3. **Scheduling Coordination** - Multiple patients, caregivers, time slots
4. **After-Hours Coverage** - Emergencies don't wait for business hours
5. **Audit Preparation** - HHS audits can be intensive

## Competitive Landscape in Texas

- Most agencies use manual systems or basic scheduling software
- Few have AI voice agents yet
- Dialora and similar starting to market to this segment
- **First-mover advantage still available in home health vertical**
