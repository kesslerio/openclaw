## The No-Show Problem

### Industry Statistics

**No-Show Rate:**

- Industry average: **22%** (1 in 5 visits missed)
- Best-in-class agencies: 8-12%
- Worst agencies: 30-40%

**Cost Per No-Show:**

- Lost revenue: $150-300 per visit
- Admin time to fix: 30-60 min ($12-25)
- Client dissatisfaction: Hard to quantify (lost future revenue)
- **Total cost per no-show:** $175-350

**Annual Cost Per Agency:**

- Small agency (500 visits/mo): $11,550-23,100/year
- Medium agency (1,000 visits/mo): $46,200-92,400/year
- Large agency (3,000 visits/mo): $138,600-277,200/year

**Total Industry Cost:**

- 33,000 agencies × avg $60,000/year = **$1.98 billion/year**
- (Conservative estimate, likely closer to $5-7 billion when including indirect costs)

---

### Root Causes of No-Shows

**Top 5 Reasons (from agency surveys):**

1. **Caregiver burnout** (working 50+ hrs/week): 35%
2. **Transportation issues** (car broke down, no gas): 25%
3. **Last-minute schedule changes** (forgot, double-booked): 20%
4. **Poor communication** (didn't see text, missed call): 12%
5. **Health issues** (caregiver got sick): 8%

**Key Insight:** 80% of no-shows are preventable with early intervention.

---

### Current Solutions (What Agencies Try)

**1. Manual Confirmation Calls (60% of agencies)**

- Call caregivers 24 hours before shift
- Effectiveness: 15-20% reduction in no-shows
- Cost: 80+ hours/month (admin time)
- Problem: Time-consuming, reactive (call after they say yes, they still no-show)

**2. Text Message Reminders (40% of agencies)**

- Automated SMS 24 hours + 2 hours before
- Effectiveness: 10-15% reduction
- Cost: $50-100/month (Twilio)
- Problem: 40% of texts ignored (especially older caregivers)

**3. Incentive Programs (20% of agencies)**

- Bonuses for perfect attendance
- Effectiveness: 5-10% reduction
- Cost: $500-2,000/month (bonus payouts)
- Problem: Expensive, doesn't address root causes

**4. Backup Caregiver Pools (15% of agencies)**

- On-call staff ready to fill in
- Effectiveness: Mitigates impact (doesn't prevent)
- Cost: High (pay people to be on standby)
- Problem: Hard to recruit/retain backups

**Gap:** No one is using predictive AI. This is our opportunity.
