## 📈 Market Trends (2026)

### 1. Voice AI is Commoditizing

- Platforms like Twilio, Vapi, Bland AI make it easy to build voice agents
- Differentiation will come from **vertical specialization**, not technology

### 2. Compliance is Table Stakes

- HIPAA, SOC 2, GDPR are minimum requirements
- Winners will be those with **industry-specific compliance** (EVV for home health)

### 3. Integration is King

- Generic solutions require custom integrations
- Turnkey solutions with **pre-built integrations win**

### 4. Vertical Solutions Beat Platforms

- "Build your own" platforms lose to "works day one" vertical solutions
- Home health agencies don't have tech resources to configure platforms
