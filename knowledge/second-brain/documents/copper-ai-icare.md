## iCare Product

### What It Does

AI voice agents that handle phone calls for home health agencies:

- Intake calls from patients/families
- Appointment scheduling
- Care coordination
- Follow-up calls
- After-hours support

### Target Market

- Home health agencies
- Home care providers
- Healthcare organizations needing 24/7 phone support

### Value Proposition

1. **24/7 Availability** - Never miss a call
2. **Cost Reduction** - Fraction of the cost of human staff
3. **Consistency** - Same quality every call
4. **Scalability** - Handle multiple calls simultaneously
5. **Integration** - Works with existing healthcare systems
