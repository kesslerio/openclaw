## Presentation Tips

### Before Pitch:

1. **Know your audience:** Research the firm, partners, thesis
2. **Tailor deck:** Highlight what they care about (market, tech, team, etc.)
3. **Practice:** 15-minute version, 30-minute deep-dive
4. **Anticipate questions:** Have appendix slides ready

### During Pitch:

1. **Tell a story:** Start with the problem (Sarah's story)
2. **Show, don't tell:** Demo the product live (or video)
3. **Be confident but coachable:** You know home health, they know investing
4. **Handle objections calmly:** "Great question, here's how we think about that..."

### After Pitch:

1. **Follow up within 24 hours:** Thank you, deck, next steps
2. **Provide data room:** Financials, customer references, contracts
3. **Move quickly:** Momentum matters
4. **Keep other investors warm:** Create FOMO
