## Phase 4: Negotiate Terms (Weeks 16-20)

### 🎯 Goal: Lock in partnership agreement that accelerates Copper AI growth

### Partnership Model Options

#### Option A: Marketplace Listing (Lowest Commitment)

**What It Is:**

- Copper AI listed in ClearCare partner directory
- Co-marketing (blog post, webinar, email to 4,500 agencies)
- No revenue share, no exclusivity

**Pros:**

- Easiest to secure
- Instant distribution to 4,500 agencies
- Validates Copper AI as "ClearCare-approved"

**Cons:**

- WellSky has no skin in the game (may not promote actively)
- Copper AI does all the work (sales, onboarding, support)

**Ideal For:** If WellSky is lukewarm, start here and prove value

#### Option B: Revenue Share (Aligned Incentives)

**What It Is:**

- Copper AI pays WellSky 10-20% of subscription revenue
- WellSky actively promotes Copper AI (sales team incentivized)
- Native API integration (WellSky provides access)

**Pros:**

- WellSky has financial incentive to promote
- Native integration = better UX, faster adoption
- Scales automatically (more customers = more revenue for both)

**Cons:**

- 10-20% margin hit (reduces Copper AI profit)
- WellSky may demand exclusivity (can't partner with Axxess)

**Ideal For:** If WellSky wants partnership but not acquisition

**Sample Terms:**

- **Revenue Share:** 15% of Copper AI subscriptions from ClearCare agencies
- **Minimum Guarantee:** $10k/year to WellSky (even if no sales)
- **Term:** 3 years, auto-renew
- **Exclusivity:** None (Copper AI can partner with other EMRs)
- **Co-Marketing:** Quarterly webinars, joint case studies
- **API Access:** WellSky provides full API access, documentation, sandbox

#### Option C: White-Label (WellSky as Distributor)

**What It Is:**

- WellSky sells Copper AI as "ClearCare Voice AI"
- WellSky handles sales, billing, support
- Revenue split: 50/50 or 60/40 (WellSky gets larger share)

**Pros:**

- WellSky sales team sells it (10x distribution power)
- Copper AI becomes embedded in ClearCare platform
- Higher total revenue (even with lower margin)

**Cons:**

- Lose control of brand, pricing, roadmap
- WellSky could eventually build competing feature
- Lower margin per customer (50/50 split)

**Ideal For:** If WellSky wants to "own" the relationship with agencies

**Sample Terms:**

- **Revenue Split:** 50/50 (WellSky gets half, Copper AI gets half)
- **Branding:** "Powered by Copper AI" (small print)
- **Pricing:** WellSky sets price (suggest $500-800/month)
- **Support:** WellSky handles Tier 1, Copper AI handles Tier 2 (technical)
- **Roadmap:** Joint product committee (both sides vote on features)

#### Option D: Acquisition (Exit Strategy)

**What It Is:**

- WellSky acquires Copper AI outright
- Arvind gets cash + equity + employment agreement
- Copper AI becomes ClearCare Voice AI (WellSky product)

**Pros:**

- Immediate liquidity ($20-50M valuation range)
- Instant distribution to 4,500 agencies
- WellSky resources (sales, eng, support)

**Cons:**

- Lose control of company
- May be forced into earnout (pay over 3-5 years)
- Could be acqui-hired (WellSky wants team, not product)

**Ideal For:** If Arvind wants to exit or WellSky is aggressive

**Valuation Framework:**

- **Current ARR:** ~$100k (estimate)
- **Multiple:** 15-25x ARR (early stage SaaS)
- **Valuation:** $1.5-2.5M (current)
- **With ClearCare proof:** $10-20M (10% penetration = $2.7M ARR)
- **With 200 ClearCare customers:** $30-50M (strategic value)

**Negotiation Leverage:**

- Competing offers (Axxess, AlayaCare)
- Customer demand (agencies requesting it)
- No-compete clause (don't build for other EMRs)

1. **Customer Pull:** Agencies are requesting it (proof: emails to WellSky CSMs)
2. **Proven ROI:** 40% no-show reduction = $6k/month savings
3. **First Mover:** No voice AI competitors in home health
4. **Competitive Threat:** "If we partner with Axxess instead, ClearCare agencies will switch"
5. **Low Integration Cost:** Already built Zapier version, easy to go native

### What WellSky Wants (Their Priorities)

1. **Revenue:** New revenue stream (15% of $500/mo × 2,000 agencies = $1.8M/year)
2. **Retention:** Agencies love ClearCare → less churn
3. **Differentiation:** "Only EMR with voice AI" (competitive advantage vs Axxess)
4. **Low Risk:** Proven solution (not experimental)
5. **Customer Success:** Agencies save money → happy customers

**Arvind:** "Great question. Our pilots show $6,000/month in savings from no-show reduction alone. So agencies see 12:1 ROI. Even at $500/month, they're saving $5,500 net. We've had zero price resistance. If anything, we could charge more. But $500 feels fair given the value."

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk #1: WellSky Says No

**Mitigation:**

- Pivot to Axxess (7,000 agencies, bigger market)
- Build proof with Axxess, then come back to WellSky with FOMO
- "Your competitor is partnering with us. Last chance."

### Risk #2: WellSky Builds Competing Feature

**Mitigation:**

- File provisional patent on "voice-first EVV" (defensive)
- Sign partnership with non-compete clause (they can't build if we partner)
- Move fast — lock in 100-200 customers before they can copy

### Risk #3: Pilots Fail (Poor ROI)

**Mitigation:**

- Choose pilot agencies carefully (high no-show rates = bigger impact)
- Over-support pilots (white-glove service, weekly check-ins)
- If one fails, replace with new agency (keep trying until 5 successes)

### Risk #4: WellSky Demands Exclusivity (No Axxess)

**Mitigation:**

- Counter with "preferred partner" (12-month head start, not exclusivity)
- Build leverage by showing Axxess interest
- Walk away if terms are bad (Axxess is bigger anyway)
