# NTTA Dispute Call Script

**Phone:** 972-229-8949  
**Purpose:** Dispute toll charges  
**Task:** #29  
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes

---

## 📋 Before You Call

### Gather These Details:

- [ ] **Account Number** (if you have one)
- [ ] **License Plate Number**
- [ ] **Date(s) of disputed toll(s)**
- [ ] **Amount(s) being disputed**
- [ ] **Reason for dispute** (incorrect charge, didn't use tollway, TollTag malfunction, etc.)
- [ ] **Supporting Evidence:**
  - Receipts showing you were elsewhere
  - TollTag transaction history
  - Photos of vehicle/plate
  - Payment confirmation if already paid

---

## 🗣️ Call Script

### Opening

**NTTA:** "Thank you for calling NTTA Customer Service. How can I help you?"

**YOU:** "Hi, I'm calling to dispute a toll charge on my account. Can you help me with that?"

---

### Account Verification

**NTTA:** "Sure, I can help. Can I have your account number or license plate?"

**YOU:** "My license plate is [LICENSE PLATE] / My account number is [ACCOUNT NUMBER]."

**NTTA:** "Thank you. And can I have your name and address for verification?"

**YOU:** "[YOUR NAME], address is [YOUR ADDRESS]."

---

### State Your Dispute

**NTTA:** "How can I help you today?"

**YOU:** _Choose the scenario that applies:_

#### Scenario 1: Incorrect Charge

"I'm calling to dispute a charge from [DATE] for [$AMOUNT]. I have a TollTag and it should have been charged at the TollTag rate, but I was charged the ZipCash rate."

#### Scenario 2: Never Used Tollway

"I'm disputing a charge from [DATE] for [$AMOUNT]. I didn't use the tollway on that date—I was [location/reason]. I believe this is an error."

#### Scenario 3: TollTag Malfunction

"I have a TollTag, but I received a ZipCash invoice for [DATE]. My TollTag should have worked. Can you check if there was a malfunction?"

#### Scenario 4: Already Paid

"I'm disputing this charge because I already paid it on [DATE]. My confirmation number is [NUMBER]."

#### Scenario 5: Excessive Late Fees

"I received the original toll of [$X], but it's now showing [$Y] with late fees. I never received the first notice. Can we remove the late fees?"

---

### Provide Evidence

**NTTA:** "Can you provide more details?"

**YOU:** "Yes, here's what I have:"

- "I have receipts showing I was at [LOCATION] at that time."
- "I have my TollTag transaction history showing it was working that day."
- "I checked my mail and never received the first notice."
- "I can email you [DOCUMENTATION] if needed."

---

### Request Resolution

**YOU:** "Based on this, I'd like to request:"

- "The charge to be removed entirely."
- "The charge to be corrected to the TollTag rate."
- "The late fees to be waived."
- "A credit to my account for the incorrect charge."

---

### If They Deny

**NTTA:** "I'm sorry, but we can't remove this charge."

**YOU:** _Stay calm and professional_

"I understand. Can you explain why it can't be removed?"

**[Listen to their reason]**

**Then:**

- "Is there a supervisor I can speak with?"
- "Can I submit a formal written dispute?"
- "What documentation would you need to reconsider?"
- "Can I request a review of the camera footage/transaction?"

---

### Get a Reference Number

**YOU:** "Before we end, can I get a reference number or case number for this dispute?"

**NTTA:** "Your case number is [NUMBER]."

**YOU:** "Thank you. What are the next steps, and when should I expect a response?"

---

### Closing

**YOU:** "Thank you for your help today. Just to confirm:"

- "You've opened a dispute for [DETAILS]."
- "My case number is [NUMBER]."
- "I should hear back within [TIMEFRAME]."
- "Is there anything else I need to do?"

**NTTA:** "[Response]"

**YOU:** "Great, thank you so much. Have a good day!"

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Dos:

✅ Be polite and professional (even if frustrated)  
✅ Have all documentation ready before calling  
✅ Write down the case number immediately  
✅ Ask for supervisor if first rep can't help  
✅ Follow up in writing (email) after the call

### Don'ts:

❌ Don't argue or raise your voice  
❌ Don't hang up without a case number  
❌ Don't accept "no" without asking why  
❌ Don't miss your follow-up deadline  
❌ Don't forget to document the call

---

## 🔄 Escalation Path

If the dispute is denied:

1. **Request Supervisor Review** (during call)
2. **Submit Written Dispute** (via NTTA website or mail)
3. **Request Administrative Review** (formal appeal)
4. **Contact County Commissioner** (if still unresolved)
5. **Small Claims Court** (last resort for large amounts)
