## 🎯 Targeting Strategy

### Find Texas Agencies

**Data Sources:**

1. **Home Care Pulse Directory**
   - Filter: Texas + 10-50 caregivers
   - Exports: Company name, owner, phone, email

2. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator**
   - Search: "Operations Manager" + "Home Care" + "Texas"
   - Filter: Dallas, Houston, Austin, San Antonio, Fort Worth
   - Export: Names, titles, companies, emails

3. **Texas HHSC Licensed Providers**
   - Public database: hhs.texas.gov
   - All licensed home health agencies
   - Name, address, license #

4. **Google Maps**
   - Search: "Home health care [City], TX"
   - Scrape: Name, phone, website
   - Tool: Instant Data Scraper (Chrome extension)

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Best Fit:**

- 10-30 caregivers (small enough to onboard, big enough to see ROI)
- Private-pay or Medicaid (commercial insurance = better margins)
- Owner-operated (decision-maker accessible)
- Currently using ClearCare or Axxess (integration story)
- Located in: Dallas, Fort Worth, Austin (Arvind can visit)

**Red Flags (Skip These):**

- Franchise corporate HQ (too many decision-makers)
- Medicaid-only (razor-thin margins)
- <5 caregivers (too small, ROI unclear)
- > 100 caregivers (enterprise sales cycle too long)

## 🎯 Personalization Variables

**Always Personalize:**

1. **[First Name]** - Use first name only (not "Mr./Mrs.")
2. **[Agency Name]** - Exact name from website
3. **[City/Area]** - Be specific ("North Dallas" not "Dallas")
4. **[Current Pain]** - If you know their pain point, mention it
   - "I saw on your website you're hiring caregivers..." (turnover)
   - "I noticed you have 15 Yelp reviews..." (small agency, owner-operated)

**Merge Tags for Bulk Send:**

- `{{firstName}}`
- `{{agencyName}}`
- `{{city}}`
- `{{state}}`
