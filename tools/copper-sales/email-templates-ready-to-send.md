## Pro Tips for Email Outreach

1. **Personalize the first line** - Reference their city, agency size, or specific pain point
2. **Keep it scannable** - Use bullet points, bold text, white space
3. **One CTA (Call-To-Action)** - Don't give them 5 options, give them ONE
4. **Send at optimal times:**
   - Tuesday-Thursday
   - 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM
   - Avoid Monday morning and Friday afternoon
5. **Use plain text** - No fancy formatting (looks more personal)
6. **Track opens/clicks** - Use email tracking to know when to follow up
7. **A/B test subject lines** - See what gets more opens
