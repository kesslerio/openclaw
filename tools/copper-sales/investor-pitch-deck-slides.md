## Presentation Tips

### Delivery

- **Time:** 10 minutes for pitch, 5 minutes for Q&A
- **Pace:** 40 seconds per slide (don't rush, but keep moving)
- **Energy:** High energy on problem/solution, calm/confident on financials
- **Eye contact:** Make eye contact, don't read slides

### Anticipated Questions

**Q: What's your biggest risk?**
A: "Competition from the EMR companies themselves. That's why we're moving fast to lock in partnerships with WellSky and Axxess. We're also patenting our no-show predictor algorithm."

**Q: Why can't ClearCare or Axxess build this?**
A: "They could, but they won't. They're platform companies focused on comprehensive EMR functionality. We're a point solution focused entirely on one problem. Plus, large companies move slowly - it would take them 18-24 months to build what we've already built."

**Q: How defensible is your technology?**
A: "Three layers of defensibility: One, we're patenting the no-show prediction algorithm. Two, we have exclusive partnerships with EMRs. Three, data network effects - the more agencies we have, the better our predictions, which makes us stickier."

**Q: What's your customer acquisition strategy?**
A: "Three channels: Direct outreach via LinkedIn and cold email, content marketing to establish thought leadership, and partnerships with WellSky and Axxess to tap into their 11,500 customer base. Our CAC is $1,200 with a 2-month sales cycle."

**Q: Why is your LTV/CAC so high?**
A: "Two reasons: Low CAC because we have a fast sales cycle and clear ROI proof, and high LTV because agencies don't churn when we're saving them $8,000+ per month. Once they see the value, they stay forever."
