## 📊 Email Performance Tracking

**Metrics to Track:**

| Metric                 | Target | How to Measure              |
| ---------------------- | ------ | --------------------------- |
| **Open Rate**          | >25%   | Email platform analytics    |
| **Click Rate**         | >5%    | Link tracking (Bitly, etc.) |
| **Reply Rate**         | >10%   | Manual count                |
| **Demo Booked**        | >5%    | Calendar bookings           |
| **Trial Signup**       | >3%    | CRM tracking                |
| **Conversion to Paid** | >25%   | Trials → Paid customers     |

**A/B Testing Ideas:**

- Subject line: Question vs. Statement vs. FOMO
- CTA: "Reply" vs. "Book Calendar" vs. "Watch Demo"
- Length: Short (100 words) vs. Long (300 words)
- Tone: Formal vs. Casual

## 🚀 Pro Tips

**1. Personalize the First Line**
Don't just use {{FirstName}}. Mention something specific:

- "I saw {{AgencyName}} is hiring on Indeed..."
- "Noticed you recently expanded to [City]..."
- "Congrats on the 5-star review on Google!"

**2. Use Video**
Embed a personalized Loom video in the email:

- "Hey {{FirstName}}, recorded a quick 90-second video showing how Copper AI works with ClearCare..."
- Higher engagement than text-only emails

**3. Follow Up on LinkedIn**
After sending email:

- Send connection request on LinkedIn
- Comment on their posts
- Build relationship (not just cold email)

**4. Track Everything**
Use a CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, etc.) to track:

- Who opened
- Who clicked
- Who replied
- Who booked demo
- Where they are in the funnel

**5. Iterate Based on Data**
After 50 emails, analyze:

- Which subject lines got the best open rates?
- Which CTAs got the most replies?
- What objections are you hearing?
- Adjust templates accordingly
