## 🎨 Design Recommendations

### Visual Style

**Color Palette:**

- Primary: Deep Blue (#003D82) - Trust, professionalism
- Secondary: Teal (#00A3A1) - Healthcare, innovation
- Accent: Orange (#FF6B35) - Energy, action
- Neutral: Light Gray (#F5F5F5), Dark Gray (#333333)

**Fonts:**

- Headlines: Montserrat Bold (modern, clean)
- Body: Open Sans Regular (readable, professional)

**Imagery:**

- Photos of real caregivers (diverse, relatable)
- Clean icons (line-style, not heavy)
- Minimal text per slide (visuals > words)

---

### Presentation Tips

**Storytelling Arc:**

1. **Problem:** Paint the pain (agencies drowning)
2. **Market:** Show the opportunity (huge, growing)
3. **Solution:** Reveal the product (hero moment)
4. **Proof:** Show it works (traction, metrics)
5. **Vision:** Where we're going (market leadership)
6. **Ask:** Join us (Series A)

**Slide Timing:**

- Total: 15-20 minutes presentation
- Leave 10-15 minutes for Q&A
- ~1-2 minutes per slide

**Delivery:**

- Start with a story (real agency owner's pain)
- Use customer quotes liberally
- Demo the product (live or video)
- End with vision + clear ask

## ✅ Pre-Pitch Checklist

### Materials to Prepare

- [ ] Update pitch deck with latest metrics
- [ ] Prepare live demo (or recorded video backup)
- [ ] Print leave-behind (1-page executive summary)
- [ ] Customer reference list (3-5 agencies willing to take calls)
- [ ] Financial model (Excel/Google Sheets)
- [ ] Data room (Dropbox/Google Drive)
  - [ ] Incorporation docs
  - [ ] Customer contracts
  - [ ] Financial statements
  - [ ] Cap table
  - [ ] IP assignments

### Rehearsal

- [ ] Practice pitch 5+ times (alone)
- [ ] Record yourself (watch for pacing, filler words)
- [ ] Pitch to friendly audience (advisors, mentors)
- [ ] Anticipate Q&A (prepare answers to tough questions)
- [ ] Memorize key stats (market size, traction, projections)

### Day-Of

- [ ] Arrive 15 minutes early
- [ ] Test AV equipment (laptop, clicker, HDMI)
- [ ] Have backup (PDF on phone, printed deck)
- [ ] Bring business cards
- [ ] Breathe, smile, own the room
