## 💬 Sales Scripts

### Opening (Discovery)

**Question:**

> "Are you currently using any voice AI for patient or caregiver communication?"

**If They Mention Hippocratic AI:**

> "Great! Hippocratic AI is a solid choice for hospitals. But as a home health agency, do they integrate with your EVV system? That's critical for Medicaid billing compliance."

**Common Answer:** "No, they don't."

**Your Response:**

> "That's the difference. Hippocratic AI is built for hospitals—appointment scheduling, chronic disease management. iCare is built specifically for home health agencies—EVV integration, caregiver workflows, visit verification. It's the vertical solution."

---

### Objection Handling

#### Objection 1: "Hippocratic AI says they're HIPAA-compliant too."

**Response:**

> "Absolutely, HIPAA compliance is table stakes for any healthcare AI. The difference is what happens _after_ compliance. Do they integrate with Axxess, ClearCare, or WellSky for EVV verification? Do they have caregiver shift reminders? Do they auto-generate visit notes for billing? That's where iCare's vertical focus makes the difference."

#### Objection 2: "Hippocratic AI has more features."

**Response:**

> "More features, or the _right_ features? If you're a hospital managing hundreds of specialties, Hippocratic AI's breadth makes sense. But for home health agencies, you need depth in EVV integration, caregiver workflows, and visit verification. That's our entire focus. We're not trying to do everything—we're trying to be the best at home health."

#### Objection 3: "They're a well-funded company with big-name investors."

**Response:**

> "That's true, and it's a testament to the voice AI market's potential. But well-funded companies often target large enterprise customers with 6-figure contracts. iCare serves home health agencies of all sizes, starting at $500/month. We're accessible to the agencies that need this technology most."

#### Objection 4: "Can you add chronic disease management like they have?"

**Response:**

> "We could, but should we? Our philosophy is to be exceptional at what home health agencies actually need—EVV compliance, caregiver management, visit verification. Chronic disease management is valuable, but it's not the #1 pain point for home health operations. We'd rather be 10/10 at EVV than 6/10 at everything."

---

### Closing

**When They're Comparing Options:**

> "Here's the bottom line: Hippocratic AI is a great product for hospitals. But you're not a hospital—you're a home health agency. You have unique needs: EVV compliance, caregiver shift confirmations, visit verification, home health billing. That's not an add-on for us—it's our entire product. We're the vertical solution built for your world."

**Call to Action:**

> "Let me show you a demo focused specifically on EVV integration and caregiver workflows. You'll see the difference between a general-purpose tool and a purpose-built solution. Does [time] work for a 15-minute walkthrough?"
