## Key Success Factors

### What Made It Work

1. **Leadership buy-in:** [Decision-Maker Name] championed the project
2. **Clear communication:** Announced rollout at staff meeting, emphasized benefits
3. **Simple adoption:** Just call a number - no app, no training needed
4. **Quick wins:** Prevented 3 no-shows in Week 1, built team confidence
5. **Weekly check-ins:** Copper AI team responsive to feedback, made adjustments

### Lessons Learned

**Challenge:** Some older caregivers skeptical of AI  
**Solution:** Emphasized it's "just a phone call" - they already know how to use phones

**Challenge:** A few caregivers forgot the number  
**Solution:** Printed wallet cards with number + instructions, laminated them

**Challenge:** Initial false alarms on risk scores (Week 1-2)  
**Solution:** AI learned agency patterns, accuracy improved to 87% by Week 4
