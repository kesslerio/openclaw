### 0:05-0:15 - THE NO-SHOW PROBLEM (10 minutes)

**[Slide 3: The $65k Problem]**  
_Headline:_ "No-Shows Cost Agencies $65,000/Year (And That's Just the Beginning)"

**Arvind:**

> Let me show you the real cost of no-shows. This is the math most agencies don't do.
>
> **Let's say you have 50 caregivers.**
>
> - Each caregiver does 20 visits per week = 1,000 visits/week total
> - Industry average no-show rate: 25%
> - That's 250 missed visits every single week
> - At $125 revenue per visit, you're losing $31,250/week
> - **Annual loss: $1.625 million**
>
> That's 4% of your total revenue walking out the door.
>
> But wait—it gets worse.

**[Slide 4: The Hidden Costs]**

**Arvind:**

> The $1.6M loss is just missed revenue. Here are the hidden costs:
>
> **1. Scheduler Time:**
>
> - Your scheduler spends 15+ hours/week calling caregivers to confirm visits
> - At $18/hour, that's $14,040/year in wasted labor
>
> **2. Caregiver Morale:**
>
> - When Maria shows up and the visit was already canceled, she's frustrated
> - Good caregivers quit because of scheduling chaos
> - Turnover costs you $5,000-10,000 per caregiver replacement
>
> **3. Patient Satisfaction:**
>
> - Mrs. Johnson waited all morning for Maria to arrive
> - When Maria doesn't show, Mrs. Johnson calls to complain
> - Star ratings drop, referrals decrease
>
> **4. Compliance Risk:**
>
> - Medicaid requires EVV for every visit
> - No-shows = missing EVV data = audit flags
> - Penalty: $10,000 per violation
>
> **Total hidden costs: $50,000-100,000/year**
>
> So the real cost of no-shows isn't $65k—it's closer to **$150k-200k/year** for a 50-caregiver agency.

**[Slide 5: Why Current Solutions Fail]**

**Arvind:**

> Most agencies try 3 things to fix no-shows:
>
> **1. Manual Confirmation Calls**
>
> - Scheduler calls 200 caregivers per week
> - Time-consuming (15+ hours/week)
> - Reactive (you're confirming, not predicting)
>
> **2. SMS Reminders**
>
> - "Hi Maria, you have a visit tomorrow at 9 AM"
> - One-way communication (Maria can't respond)
> - Doesn't address the root cause (why Maria no-shows)
>
> **3. EVV Mobile Apps**
>
> - Download the app, log in, check in/out
> - Adoption problem: 40% of caregivers don't have smartphones
> - Forgotten passwords, dead batteries, app crashes
>
> **The result? None of these work.**
>
> Why? Because they're all **reactive**. You're chasing problems after they happen, not preventing them.

**[Slide 6: The Breakthrough]**

**Arvind:**

> What if you could predict which visits will be no-shows **24 hours before they happen**?
>
> And then intervene proactively—call the caregiver, offer support, prevent the problem before it starts.
>
> That's the breakthrough. And that's what we built at Copper AI.

#### STEP 1: PREDICT NO-SHOWS (7 minutes)

**[Slide 8: How Machine Learning Works]**

**Arvind:**

> Step 1 is prediction. We use machine learning to analyze 50+ factors and predict which visits are at risk.
>
> **Here are the top 10 factors:**
>
> 1. **Caregiver history:** Has Maria missed visits before? How often is she late?
> 2. **Travel distance:** Is this visit 20+ miles away? (Long drives = higher no-show risk)
> 3. **Weather:** Is it raining or snowing tomorrow? (2x no-show rate in bad weather)
> 4. **Day of week:** Friday afternoons are 3x riskier than Tuesday mornings
> 5. **Time of day:** Early morning (6 AM) and late evening (8 PM) have higher no-show rates
> 6. **Patient complexity:** Difficult patients = higher caregiver turnover
> 7. **Caregiver tenure:** New caregivers (<30 days) are 2x more likely to no-show
> 8. **Traffic patterns:** Rush hour drives increase no-show risk
> 9. **Caregiver availability:** Did Maria just work 6 days straight? (Burnout risk)
> 10. **Last-minute assignments:** Visits scheduled <24 hours ahead are risky
>
> Our ML model combines all 50 factors and generates a **risk score** for every visit:
>
> - **Green (0-30%):** Low risk—probably fine
> - **Yellow (30-60%):** Moderate risk—keep an eye on it
> - **Red (60-100%):** High risk—intervene NOW

#### STEP 3: VOICE EVV (3 minutes)

**[Slide 12: Voice-Based Check-Ins]**

**Arvind:**

> Step 3: Make check-ins effortless.
>
> Here's the problem with EVV apps:
>
> - 40% of caregivers don't have smartphones
> - Forgotten passwords
> - Dead batteries
> - App crashes
>
> **Our solution:** Voice-based EVV.
>
> When Maria arrives at Mrs. Johnson's house, she calls a toll-free number from **any phone**:
>
> - Her personal phone
> - Mrs. Johnson's landline
> - A flip phone
>
> [Play 15-second audio]
>
> **AI:** "Hi, this is Copper AI. Please say your name and the patient's name to check in."
>
> **Maria:** "This is Maria, I'm with Mrs. Johnson."

>
> [Audio ends]

**Arvind:**

> That's it. No app, no tablet, no password.
>
> The check-in time syncs to your EHR (ClearCare or Axxess) automatically. Zero manual entry.
>
> **Result:** 100% caregiver adoption (vs 60% for apps).

**Arvind:**

> Let's put it all together:
>
> **Step 1:** Every morning at 6 AM, Copper AI pulls your schedule from ClearCare/Axxess and flags risky visits.
>
> **Step 2:** At 10 AM, the AI calls high-risk caregivers: "Confirming you're all set for tomorrow."
>
> **Step 3:** When caregivers arrive, they call to check in. The timestamp logs to your EHR instantly.
>
> **Result:** 45% reduction in no-shows, 100% EVV compliance, 12+ hours/week saved for schedulers.
