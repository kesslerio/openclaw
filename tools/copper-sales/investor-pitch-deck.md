## SLIDE 4: HOW IT WORKS

### Simple, Automated, Invisible

**Step 1: Sync Schedule**  
Every morning, Copper AI pulls your schedule from ClearCare or Axxess automatically.

**Step 2: Predict Risk**  
ML model analyzes 50+ factors:

- Caregiver history (late check-ins, no-shows)
- Travel distance & traffic
- Weather conditions
- Day/time patterns

**Step 3: Proactive Call**  
At-risk visits get a friendly AI call 24 hours ahead:  
_"Hi Maria, this is Copper AI. Just confirming you're all set for Mrs. Johnson tomorrow at 9 AM. Need directions?"_

**Step 4: Voice EVV**  
When Maria arrives, she calls in:  
_"I'm here at Mrs. Johnson's."_  
AI logs check-in to ClearCare instantly. No app, no tablet, no manual entry.

**Step 5: Analytics Dashboard**  
Agencies see:

- No-show rate trends
- Caregiver reliability scores
- ROI metrics (recovered visits, time saved)
