# ✅ Demo Success Checklist

**Goal:** Turn demos into closed deals. This checklist ensures you nail every demo.

---

## 📋 Pre-Demo (1 Hour Before)

### Research the Agency

- [ ] Check their website - note agency size, services offered
- [ ] LinkedIn - find decision maker's background
- [ ] Google Reviews - understand their reputation/pain points
- [ ] Competitors - see what other agencies in their area use

### Prepare Personalization

- [ ] Know their agency name (practice saying it)
- [ ] Estimate their agency size (# caregivers, # clients)
- [ ] Pre-calculate their ROI (use ROI calculator)
- [ ] Have 1-2 relevant pain points ready to mention

### Technical Setup

- [ ] Test Zoom audio/video
- [ ] Have demo environment ready
- [ ] Open EVV dashboard (demo mode)
- [ ] Have pricing sheet ready
- [ ] Calendar link ready to book follow-up

### Send Confirmation Email

- [ ] 1 hour before: "Looking forward to our demo at [Time]"
- [ ] Include Zoom link again
- [ ] Mention what you'll cover (build anticipation)

---

## 🎯 During Demo (5-10 Minutes)

### Opening (1 min)

- [ ] **Greet warmly:** "Hi [Name]! Thanks for making time. How's your day going?"
- [ ] **Small talk:** 30 seconds of rapport building
- [ ] **Set agenda:** "I'll show you the system in 5 minutes, then answer any questions. Sound good?"
- [ ] **Ask discovery question:** "Before we dive in, quick question - what's your biggest pain point with EVV or scheduling right now?"

**Key:** Get them talking about their pain FIRST!

### Demo Flow (5-7 minutes)

**1. The Caregiver Experience (2 min)**

- [ ] Show: Caregiver calls the number
- [ ] Show: AI answers, recognizes voice
- [ ] Show: "Clock in for your 2pm visit with Mrs. Johnson?"
- [ ] Show: GPS captured, timestamp recorded
- [ ] **Ask:** "See how easy that is? No app to download or remember."

**2. The Admin Dashboard (2 min)**

- [ ] Show: Real-time EVV compliance dashboard
- [ ] Show: All visits logged automatically
- [ ] Show: Export for state reporting (CMS compliant)
- [ ] **Tie to pain:** "Remember you mentioned [their pain]? This solves that."

**3. Additional Features (1 min)**

- [ ] Show: Automated visit confirmations
- [ ] Show: Caregiver Q&A (24/7 AI answering)
- [ ] Show: No-show reduction (call/text reminders)

**4. The ROI Slide (1 min)**

- [ ] Show: Their personalized ROI calculation
- [ ] Highlight: "$[X]/month savings vs $[Y]/month cost"
- [ ] Emphasize: "Pays for itself in 1-2 weeks"

**5. Implementation (30 sec)**

- [ ] Show: 2-week timeline
- [ ] Explain: Week 1 setup, Week 2 caregiver training, Week 3 live
- [ ] Reassure: "We handle everything - you just introduce it to your team"

### Trial Close (1 min)

- [ ] **Ask:** "Does this make sense for [Agency Name]?"
- [ ] **Listen:** Let them respond (DON'T FILL SILENCE)
- [ ] **Address concerns:** Handle objections (see objection guide)

### Next Steps (1 min)

- [ ] **If interested:** "Great! I can get you started this week. Let me send over the agreement."
- [ ] **If needs time:** "Totally understand. When do you think you'll have a decision?"
- [ ] **Book follow-up:** "Let's schedule a quick check-in for [Date/Time] to answer any questions."

### Close the Call

- [ ] Thank them for their time
- [ ] Reiterate next steps
- [ ] Send follow-up email IMMEDIATELY (within 5 min)

---

## 📧 Post-Demo (Immediately After)

### Send Thank You Email (Within 5 Minutes)

- [ ] Thank them for their time
- [ ] Recap what you showed
- [ ] Attach: Pricing sheet, case study, ROI calculator
- [ ] Include: Clear next steps
- [ ] **CTA:** "Reply YES to move forward" or "Let me know what questions you have"

### Log the Call

- [ ] Update tracker: Demo completed
- [ ] Note: Their pain points mentioned
- [ ] Note: Objections raised
- [ ] Note: Follow-up date scheduled
- [ ] Rate: Interest level (Hot/Warm/Cold)

## 📞 Follow-Up Cadence

### Day 1 (Same Day)

- [x] Send thank you email (done immediately)

### Day 3

- [ ] Send check-in email: "Any questions about what we discussed?"
- [ ] **If no response:** Call them (voicemail + email)

### Day 7

- [ ] Final follow-up: "Closing the loop - are you ready to move forward?"
- [ ] **Offer incentive:** "If you sign by Friday, I can get you live 1 week earlier"

### Day 14

- [ ] If still no response: Move to nurture list
- [ ] Send: "I'll check back in Q2 - let me know if anything changes"

## 🎯 Demo Best Practices

### DO

✅ Ask questions - make it a conversation, not a monologue
✅ Tie features to THEIR pain points
✅ Use their name frequently
✅ Smile (even on Zoom - it comes through in your voice)
✅ Show, don't tell (let them see it working)
✅ Handle objections confidently (see objection guide)
✅ Ask for the sale ("Does this make sense for you?")

### DON'T

❌ Talk for more than 2 minutes straight
❌ Use jargon ("API", "webhooks", "ML models")
❌ Rush through the demo
❌ Be defensive about price
❌ End without clear next steps
❌ Forget to follow up same day

## 🔥 Advanced Demo Techniques

### The Assumptive Close

**Don't ask IF they want it, ask WHEN:**

> "Great! I can get you started this week or next week. Which works better?"

### The Puppy Dog Close

**Offer a trial:**

> "Tell you what - let's get you set up and you can try it for 30 days. If it doesn't save you money, we'll refund it. Fair?"

### The Takeaway

**Create scarcity:**

> "I should mention - we're only onboarding 5 new agencies this month, and we have 3 slots left. If you want one, I'd recommend securing it this week."

### The Alternative Close

**Give two options, both lead to sale:**

> "Do you want the basic package at $500/month or the premium with 24/7 support at $800/month?"

### The Referral Ask

**If they're not ready:**

> "No problem! Do you know any other agencies who might benefit from this? I'd love to help them out."
