## When to Recommend Dialora (Yes, Really)

Be honest about fit. You'll build more trust by knowing when _not_ to sell.

**Recommend Dialora if:**

- Agency has <5 caregivers (too small for iCare ROI)
- They're in a different vertical (rehab, PT, urgent care)
- They explicitly only need "basic call answering" with no compliance needs
- They're already heavily invested in Dialora's integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce)

**Then say:**
"Dialora is a great fit for your current needs. When you scale beyond [X caregivers] or start dealing with EVV/Medicaid complexity, let's revisit iCare. We'll be here."
