## ✨ The Solution: Copper AI

**AI voice agents that predict and prevent no-shows before they happen.**

### 🎙️ Feature 1: Voice EVV

Caregivers call a number to clock in/out. That's it.

- ✅ **15 seconds** vs 2 minutes (8x faster)
- ✅ **Works for everyone** (even caregivers without smartphones)
- ✅ **Zero training** (just call a number)
- ✅ **Auto-updates EMR** (ClearCare, Axxess via integration)
- ✅ **HIPAA compliant** (no PHI stored on devices)

### 🔮 Feature 2: No-Show Predictor (Our Secret Sauce)

AI analyzes caregiver history, shift details, weather, and 20+ factors to predict no-show risk.

**Example Risk Assessment:**

```
🔴 RISK LEVEL: HIGH (68% probability)

Risk Factors:
  • Caregiver has 18% no-show rate (vs 8% average)
  • Working 52 hours this week (burnout risk)
  • Overnight shift (harder)
  • Snow forecast + extreme cold
  • No reliable transportation on file

Recommended Actions:
  → Call 6 hours before + 2 hours before
  → Identify backup caregiver NOW
  → Offer transportation assistance
```

**If HIGH/CRITICAL risk:** AI makes confirmation call automatically. If caregiver can't make it, you get **instant alert** with time to find backup.
