## 💰 The Opportunity

**Market Size:**

- US Home Health: **$240B** (2026) → $692B (2035), growing **12% annually**
- Total Agencies: **33,000** (and growing)
- Voice AI in Healthcare: $1.2B → $11.6B by 2034 (**37.85% CAGR**)

**Pain Points (Industry-Wide):**

- **No-shows:** 5-15% of visits = $30-60k lost revenue/year per agency
- **EVV compliance:** Federally mandated, but 15-30% error rate with mobile apps
- **Caregiver shortages:** 65-82% annual turnover, can't reach caregivers in field
- **AI adoption:** 53% of agencies investing in AI (ready buyers!)

---

## 🚀 The Solution

**What We Built:**

**Copper AI = Autonomous Operations Manager** (not just voice assistant)

**Three Core Capabilities:**

1. **Reduces No-Shows by 30%**
   - Automated confirmation calls 2 hours before shifts
   - Proactive alerts when caregivers can't make it
   - Real-time backup caregiver assignment

2. **Automates EVV Compliance (95% Accuracy)**
   - Voice-first clock in/out (hands-free, no app download)
   - Real-time error prevention ("You're 2 miles from client address, is this correct?")
   - Direct integration with EMR systems (ClearCare, Axxess)

3. **Optimizes Caregiver Workflows**
   - 24/7 voice assistant for schedule updates, visit notes
   - Predictive burnout detection (prevent turnover)
   - Multi-language support (Spanish, etc.)

**Key Differentiator:**

> "No app download. No training. Just call and talk. Works on any phone—flip phone, smartphone, doesn't matter."
