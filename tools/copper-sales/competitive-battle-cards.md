## Battle Card #1: Copper AI vs. Vapi

### Competitor Overview

**Vapi** is a generic voice AI platform for developers. Founded 2023, $10M Series A (2024). Used by call centers, real estate, e-commerce, and some healthcare.

### When Prospects Mention Vapi

**What they'll say:**

- "We're looking at Vapi—they seem more established"
- "Vapi has more features and integrations"
- "Vapi's pricing looks competitive"

### Your Response

**1. Industry Focus**

> **Prospect:** "Vapi seems like a solid option."
>
> **You:** "Vapi is a great horizontal platform—they work across many industries. But here's the challenge: home health has unique needs that generic platforms don't address.
>
> For example:
>
> - **40% of caregivers don't have smartphones.** Vapi's solution requires app downloads or web access. That won't work for nearly half your team.
> - **No-show prediction requires industry-specific data.** Vapi doesn't have 100k+ home health visits to train their ML model. We do.
> - **EHR integration matters.** Vapi doesn't have pre-built connectors for ClearCare or Axxess. You'd have to custom-build that yourself.
>
> We built Copper AI specifically for home health agencies. That's our only focus."

**2. Pricing Transparency**

> **Prospect:** "Vapi's pricing seems lower."
>
> **You:** "Great question! Vapi charges $0.10/minute (usage-based pricing). That sounds cheap until you do the math.
>
> Here's what that looks like for a 50-caregiver agency:
>
> - 250 AI calls/month × 4 minutes/call = 1,000 minutes
> - 1,000 minutes × $0.10 = $100/month
> - Plus: 1,000 voice EVV check-ins × 1 minute = 1,000 minutes
> - 1,000 minutes × $0.10 = $100/month
> - **Total: $200/month** (seems good!)
>
> But wait—what happens when you scale?
>
> - At 100 caregivers: $400/month
> - At 200 caregivers: $800/month
>
> **Copper AI is fixed at $800/month** no matter how many calls you make. Unlimited usage. Predictable costs.
>
> With Vapi, your bill grows every month. With us, it stays flat."

**3. Home Health Features**

> **Prospect:** "Can Vapi do what you do?"
>
> **You:** "Technically, yes—but you'd have to build it yourself. Here's what's included with Copper AI out-of-the-box:
>
> ✅ **ClearCare/Axxess integration** (pre-built, 15-minute setup)  
> ✅ **ML no-show prediction** (trained on 100k+ home health visits)  
> ✅ **Voice EVV compliance** (state-specific rules baked in)  
> ✅ **Risk scoring dashboard** (green/yellow/red color-coding)  
> ✅ **ROI tracking** (recovered visits, time saved, cost savings)
>
> With Vapi, you'd have to:
>
> - Build the ClearCare/Axxess integration yourself (2-4 weeks of dev work)
> - Train the ML model yourself (requires 100k+ data points you don't have)
> - Ensure EVV compliance yourself (hire a consultant to review)
>
> **Our product is turn-key.** Theirs is a developer tool.
>
> Do you have an in-house dev team to build all that? Or would you rather we handle it?"

## Battle Card #2: Copper AI vs. Bland AI

### Competitor Overview

**Bland AI** is a usage-based voice AI platform for sales and support calls. Founded 2023, bootstrapped. Popular with call centers, real estate, and B2B sales teams.

### When Prospects Mention Bland AI

**What they'll say:**

- "Bland AI is cheaper per minute"
- "They have great reviews on Product Hunt"
- "They seem easier to set up"

### Your Response

**1. Industry Fit**

> **Prospect:** "Bland AI looks simpler."
>
> **You:** "Bland AI is optimized for sales and support calls—think lead qualification, appointment setting, customer service. Great for those use cases!
>
> But home health operations are different:
>
> - **Sales calls = 1-2 minutes.** Our AI calls are 3-5 minutes (more context, more complexity).
> - **Sales calls = one-time.** Our AI calls are ongoing (200+ calls/month per agency).
> - **Sales calls don't need EVV compliance.** We do.
>
> Bland AI wasn't built for home health. We were."

**2. No-Show Prediction**

> **Prospect:** "Can Bland AI predict no-shows?"
>
> **You:** "No. Bland AI makes calls, but it doesn't predict which calls to make. You'd have to manually flag at-risk visits.
>
> With Copper AI, the ML model does this automatically:
>
> - Analyzes 50+ factors (caregiver history, distance, weather, etc.)
> - Flags risky visits every morning
> - Triggers AI calls only for at-risk visits (saves time + money)
>
> **Bland AI = reactive** (you tell it who to call).  
> **Copper AI = proactive** (we tell you who needs calling)."

**3. Pricing at Scale**

> **Prospect:** "Bland AI charges $0.09/minute—cheaper than Vapi."
>
> **You:** "True! But usage-based pricing hits you when you scale.
>
> Here's the math for a 100-caregiver agency:
>
> - 500 AI calls/month × 4 minutes = 2,000 minutes
> - 2,000 voice EVV check-ins × 1 minute = 2,000 minutes
> - **Total: 4,000 minutes × $0.09 = $360/month**
>
> Sounds good! But what happens at 200 caregivers?
>
> - 1,000 calls × 4 min = 4,000 minutes
> - 4,000 EVV check-ins × 1 min = 4,000 minutes
> - **Total: 8,000 minutes × $0.09 = $720/month**
>
> At that point, you're paying the same as Copper AI ($800/month)—but you don't get no-show prediction or EHR integrations.
>
> **Copper AI is fixed at $800/month.** Whether you have 50 caregivers or 500, the price doesn't change."

- "They work with bigger health systems—seems more established"
- "They have HIPAA compliance figured out"

**1. Market Fit**

> **Prospect:** "Dialora is also healthcare-focused."
>
> **You:** "Absolutely! Dialora is great for large health systems—think hospital call centers, multi-location clinics, enterprise-level deployments.
>
> But here's where we differ:
>
> - **Dialora targets 500+ bed hospitals.** We target 10-100 caregiver agencies.
> - **Dialora pricing starts at $5,000/month.** We start at $500/month.
> - **Dialora requires 1-month implementation.** We're live in 15 minutes.
>

**2. Home Health Specificity**

> **Prospect:** "Does Dialora do what you do?"
>
> **You:** "Dialora handles appointment scheduling, patient intake, and prescription refills—mostly for clinic-based care.
>
> Home health is different:
>
> - **Caregiver no-shows:** This isn't a problem for clinics (patients no-show, not staff). We solve caregiver-side no-shows.
> - **Voice EVV:** Clinics don't need EVV. Home health agencies do (it's federal law).
> - **Field-based operations:** Clinics have Wi-Fi and computers. Caregivers are in the field with just a phone (40% don't even have smartphones).

**3. Speed to Value**

> **Prospect:** "Dialora seems more robust."

> But here's the trade-off:
>
> - **Dialora takes 4-8 weeks to implement.** You need IT involvement, custom configs, training sessions.

> If you can wait 2 months and pay $5k+/month, Dialora is great. If you want results this week and pay $800/month, we're the better choice."

## Battle Card #4: Copper AI vs. EVV Mobile Apps (CareSmartz, Alayacare, Santrax)

### Competitor Overview

**CareSmartz, Alayacare, Santrax** are mobile app-based EVV solutions. Caregivers download an app, log in, and check in/out from their smartphones.

### When Prospects Mention EVV Apps

**What they'll say:**

- "We already have an EVV app"
- "EVV apps are cheaper ($200-300/month)"
- "Our caregivers are used to the app"

### Your Response

**1. Adoption Problem**

> **Prospect:** "We're using [EVV App]."
>
> **You:** "How's adoption going?"
>
> [Wait for response. Most will say 60-70%, some say worse]
>
> **You:** "That's common. Here's why EVV apps struggle:
>
> - **40% of caregivers don't have smartphones.** Your app doesn't work for nearly half your team.
> - **Forgotten passwords.** Caregivers get locked out weekly. Your scheduler resets passwords constantly.
> - **Battery issues.** Phones die mid-shift. Caregivers miss check-ins.
> - **App updates.** Caregivers forget to update the app. Old versions crash.
>
> **Voice EVV solves all of this:**
>
> - Works on ANY phone (smartphone, flip phone, landline, patient's phone)
> - No login required (call a number, say your name, done)
> - No battery dependency (use any available phone)
> - No updates (it's a phone call, not an app)
>
> **Result: 100% adoption** vs 60% for apps."

**2. No-Show Prevention**

> **Prospect:** "Our EVV app tracks check-ins. What more do we need?"
>
> **You:** "Great question! EVV apps are **reactive**—they log check-ins after caregivers arrive.
>
> But what about the **25% of visits that don't happen**? The app doesn't help with that.
>
> **Copper AI is proactive:**
>
> - Predicts which visits will be no-shows 24 hours ahead
> - Calls caregivers before problems happen
> - Prevents no-shows before they occur
>
> **EVV apps = compliance.**  
> **Copper AI = compliance + revenue recovery.**
>

**3. Total Cost Comparison**

> **Prospect:** "EVV apps are only $200-300/month. You're $800/month."
>
> **You:** "True! But let's look at total cost:
>
> **EVV App Only:**
>

> - 60% adoption (40% don't use it)
> - Scheduler spends 15 hours/week manually logging missed check-ins ($14,040/year in labor)
> - No-show rate stays at 25% (losing $1.625M/year)
> - **Total cost: $1.639M/year** (opportunity cost + labor)

> **Copper AI (EVV + No-Show Prevention):**

> - Scheduler saves 12 hours/week ($11,232/year saved)
> - No-show rate drops to 14% (recovering $910k/year)
> - **Net savings: $900k/year**
>
> So yes, we're $500/month more expensive. But you're saving $900k/year.
>
> **ROI: 94x.**"
