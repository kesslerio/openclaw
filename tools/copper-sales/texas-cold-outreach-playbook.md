## 📊 Objection Handling

### "We already have a system"

> "That's great! Can I ask - is it app-based or voice-based? [Answer]"
>
> "The reason I ask is we're seeing agencies switch from apps to voice because the adoption rate is so much higher. Caregivers can just call a number instead of opening an app."
>
> "Worth comparing? I can show you in 5 minutes."

### "Too expensive"

> "I totally understand. What are you paying now? [Answer]"
>
> "Our system is typically $500-800/month, but agencies your size save $6,000+ from reducing no-shows and admin time. It pays for itself in 1-2 weeks."
>
> "Want to see the ROI breakdown? I can show you exactly how the math works."

### "Not the right time"

> "Fair enough. When would be a better time? [Answer]"
>
> "Can I just ask - is it a budget thing, a timing thing, or just not a priority right now? [Answer]"
>
> **If budget:** "No problem. I can follow up in [timeframe]. For what it's worth, most agencies find the system pays for itself immediately, but I respect the timing."
>
> **If timing:** "Totally understand. Should I check back in [Month]?"
>
> **If not priority:** "Got it. Just curious - what's taking priority right now? [Could lead to other pain points]"

### "I need to talk to my team"

> "Absolutely, that makes sense. Who else would need to be involved? [Answer]"
>
> "Would it help if I did the demo with your whole team on one call? Or would you rather see it first and then bring them in?"
>
> **Book follow-up:**
> "When do you think you'll have had a chance to discuss it? I can follow up [specific day/time]."

### "Send me more information"

> "Happy to! What specifically would be helpful? [Answer]"
>
> **Then offer:**
> "I can send over a one-pager, pricing sheet, and case study. But honestly, the 5-minute demo shows it way better than any doc. Can we schedule that first, and I'll send materials after?"

### "We're too small"

> "That's actually perfect - our sweet spot is 5-20 caregivers. Smaller agencies see ROI faster because every no-show or missed call hits harder."
>
> "If you have even 5 caregivers, you're probably dealing with 20-30 calls per day. We can cut that by 60-70%."

### "We're in Missouri and don't have EVV yet"

> "You're cutting it close! The hard edits start April 1st - that's 8 weeks away. Most implementations take 2-3 weeks, so you'd want to start in February."
>
> "The good news is our system is built specifically for EVV compliance. We handle the technical requirements so you don't have to worry about it."
>
> "Want to see how it works?"

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Call early (9-10 AM)** - Decision makers are fresher, less stressed
2. **Sound like a human** - Slow down, be conversational, smile while talking
3. **Ask for the demo** - Don't just "send information", book the call
4. **Follow up fast** - Same-day email after voicemail (within 1 hour)
5. **Use their name** - Personalization matters
6. **Track everything** - What you measure improves
7. **Celebrate small wins** - Each conversation is progress
8. **Don't take rejection personally** - It's a numbers game
