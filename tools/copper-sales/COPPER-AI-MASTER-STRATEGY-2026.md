---

### Agentic AI Features (Roadmap)

**V2.0 (Q2 2026 - "Proactive Operations Manager"):**

**1. No-Show Risk Prediction**

- Analyze historical data (caregiver patterns, client history, weather, time-of-day)
- Flag high-risk visits 24-48 hours in advance
- Auto-trigger confirmation calls for high-risk visits
- **Impact:** 30-50% reduction in no-shows

**2. Caregiver Burnout Detection**

- Monitor overtime hours, last-minute shift changes, sick calls
- Detect early warning signs (3+ last-minute cancellations in 7 days)
- Alert admin: "Maria is showing burnout signs, consider schedule adjustment"
- **Impact:** 20-30% reduction in caregiver turnover

**3. EVV Error Prevention**

- Real-time validation during clock-in/out
- "You're clocking in from 2 miles away from client address, is this correct?"
- Prevent errors before claim submission
- **Impact:** 50-70% reduction in denied claims

**4. Automated Schedule Optimization**

- AI suggests optimal routes (reduce drive time)
- Identifies gaps in schedule (fill with PRN visits)
- Balances caregiver workload (prevent burnout)
- **Impact:** 15-20% efficiency gain

## 💰 Business Model

### Pricing Strategy

**Current: Flat-Rate Model**

- $500/month per agency (unlimited users, unlimited calls)
- All features included (no upsells)
- Month-to-month (no long-term contracts)

**Proposed: Tiered Model**

| Tier              | Price        | Target           | Features                                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Starter**       | $300/mo      | 1-10 caregivers  | Voice EVV, basic scheduling                                   |
| **Professional**  | $500/mo      | 11-50 caregivers | + Automated confirmations, analytics                          |
| **Enterprise**    | $1,000/mo    | 51+ caregivers   | + Predictive features, custom integrations, dedicated support |
| **Annual Prepay** | 20% discount | All tiers        | Lock-in for 12 months                                         |

**Why Tiered:**

- Captures small agencies ($300/mo accessible)
- Upsell path (Starter → Pro → Enterprise)
- Higher ARPU from large agencies ($1,000/mo vs $500/mo)

**Alternative: Per-Caregiver Pricing**

- $10/month per active caregiver
- Scales with agency size (fairer for small agencies)
- Example: 20 caregivers × $10/mo = $200/mo (more affordable than $300/mo flat)
