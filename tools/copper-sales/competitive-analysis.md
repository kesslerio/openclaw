## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors (Voice AI for Home Health)

#### 1. **Vapi** (vapi.ai)

**Positioning:** Generic voice AI platform (not healthcare-specific)

**Strengths:**

- ✅ Mature platform (2+ years in market)
- ✅ Low-code builder (non-technical users)
- ✅ Strong developer community
- ✅ Flexible API (integrate anywhere)

**Weaknesses:**

- ❌ Not healthcare-specific (no EMR integrations)
- ❌ No no-show prediction (just voice calls)
- ❌ No pre-built home health workflows
- ❌ Requires technical setup (DIY approach)

**Pricing:** $0.05-0.15 per minute (usage-based)  
**Typical cost:** $300-600/month for 50-caregiver agency

**Why Copper AI Wins:**

- ✅ Pre-built home health workflows (launch in 3 days vs 3 months)
- ✅ EMR integrations out-of-the-box (ClearCare, Axxess)
- ✅ No-show predictor (category-defining feature)
- ✅ Fixed pricing (predictable cost vs usage-based)

## Positioning Strategy

### Unique Value Proposition

**"The only voice AI platform built specifically for home health that predicts and prevents no-shows before they happen."**

### Differentiation Points

1. **No-Show Predictor** (category-defining)
   - No competitor has ML-powered no-show prediction
   - 45% reduction vs 20-30% with basic confirmations
   - This alone justifies Pro plan pricing

2. **Voice-First EVV** (accessibility)
   - 40% of home health caregivers don't have smartphones
   - Voice calls work for everyone (no app needed)
   - 8x faster than mobile app (15 sec vs 2 min)

3. **Turnkey Solution** (speed to value)
   - 3 days to launch vs 4-8 weeks for competitors
   - Pre-built EMR integrations (ClearCare, Axxess)
   - No dev work required

4. **Fixed Pricing** (predictability)
   - $500-800/month (all-inclusive)
   - No surprise bills (vs usage-based competitors)
   - Easier to budget and forecast ROI

5. **Home Health Native** (industry expertise)
   - Built by people who understand the problem
   - Workflows designed for home health operations
   - HIPAA-compliant out of the box

**Threat:** ClearCare or Axxess build competing features in-house

**Mitigation:**

- ✅ **Partnership approach:** Position as complementary add-on

**Threat:** Well-funded startup enters market (e.g., Legion pivots to home health)

**Mitigation:**

- ✅ **First-mover:** Get to 500 agencies quickly (defensible market share)
- ✅ **Customer lock-in:** Switching costs high once integrated into workflow
- ✅ **Network effects:** More agencies = better prediction accuracy

## Pricing Recommendations

### Current Pricing (Good)

- Basic: $500/month
- Pro: $800/month
- Enterprise: Custom

### Potential Adjustments

**Option 1: Tiered by agency size**

```
Small (10-30 caregivers):    $400/month
Medium (31-75 caregivers):   $700/month
Large (76+ caregivers):      $1,000/month
Enterprise (multi-location): Custom
```

**Option 2: Usage-based (risky but competitive)**

```
Base: $200/month
+ $5 per caregiver per month
+ $0.05 per confirmation call

Example: 50 caregivers, 1,000 calls/month = $200 + $250 + $50 = $500/month
```

**Option 3: Value-based (premium positioning)**

```
Basic: $800/month (no predictor)
Pro: $1,200/month (with predictor)
Enterprise: $2,000+/month (multi-location)

Justification: Agencies save $8,000-18,000/month, so $1,200 is only 6-15% of savings
```

**Nike's Recommendation:** Stick with current pricing for now. $800/month Pro plan is the sweet spot:

- High enough to signal quality
- Low enough for fast adoption
- 9-10:1 ROI is compelling
- Room to raise prices once market leader
