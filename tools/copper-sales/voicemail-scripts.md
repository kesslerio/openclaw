## Pro Tips

1. **Smile while leaving the message** - It comes through in your voice
2. **Speak slowly and clearly** - Decision makers are often older
3. **Repeat your number twice** - Once in the middle, once at the end
4. **Give them an easy next step** - "Call me" or "Book here"
5. **Send email within 5 minutes** - Strike while the iron is hot
6. **Use different scripts on different attempts** - Don't sound robotic
