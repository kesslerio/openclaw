## 💡 Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring (Show Higher Price First)

**Wrong:**

> "We have a $500 plan or an $800 plan."

**Right:**

> "Our professional plan is $800/month, but for agencies your size, the $500 starter plan should be perfect."

**Why:** Makes $500 feel like a bargain.

### ROI-Based Pricing

**Don't lead with price, lead with savings:**

> "Most agencies your size save $5-6k/month from reduced no-shows and admin time. Our system is $800/month, so you're netting $4-5k in savings."

**Why:** $800 feels cheap compared to $5,000 savings.

### Annual Discount

**Offer 2 months free for annual payment:**

- Monthly: $800/month = $9,600/year
- Annual: $8,000/year (17% discount)

**Why:** Improves cash flow and reduces churn.

## 🤝 Negotiation Guidelines

### When They Say "Too Expensive"

**1. Reframe to Daily Cost**

> "$800/month sounds like a lot, but that's only $27 per day. You probably spend more than that on coffee for your office."

**2. Show Per-Caregiver Cost**

> "For 15 caregivers, that's $53 per caregiver per month. Less than $2/day per person."

**3. Compare to Alternatives**

> "Mobile EVV apps typically cost $30-50 per user per month. That would be $600-750 for 15 caregivers. We're $800 for the whole agency, unlimited usage."

**4. Show Savings (The Closer)**

> "I totally understand budget concerns. Let me show you the math: [Pull up ROI calculator] You're currently losing $X/month to no-shows. Copper AI saves you $Y. Even if we only save you half that, you're still netting $Z profit."

### When to Discount (Rarely!)

**DO discount if:**

- ✅ Annual payment upfront (2 months free)
- ✅ Pilot customer (first 5 clients get 20% off for 6 months)
- ✅ Referral (give 1 month free for each successful referral)
- ✅ Multi-location (10% off for 3+ locations)

**DON'T discount if:**

- ❌ They just ask for a discount (train them to pay full price)
- ❌ You're desperate to close (shows weakness)
- ❌ They haven't seen the demo yet (they don't know the value)

### Discount Language

**Never say:** "I can give you a discount."  
**Instead say:** "I can offer you our pilot pricing since you're one of our first Texas agencies."

**Never say:** "How about 20% off?"  
**Instead say:** "If you commit to an annual contract today, I can do $640/month instead of $800."

## 📈 Pricing Objection Scripts

### "Your competitor charges less"

> "That's interesting - who are you comparing us to? [Listen]"
>
> "Ah, [Competitor]. They're focused on [X], whereas we're built specifically for home health. Here's the difference: [Show feature comparison]"
>
> "Also, their pricing is per-user, so at your agency size, you'd actually pay more. Let me show you the math."

### "We don't have the budget right now"

> "I totally get it. When do you typically review budgets? [Answer]"
>
> "Here's the thing though - Copper AI actually _saves_ you money. You're not adding a cost, you're reducing costs."
>
> "What if I could show you how this pays for itself in the first month? Would that change the conversation?"

### "We need to think about it"

> "Absolutely, that's smart. Can I ask - what specifically do you need to think about? [Answer]"
>
> **If price:** "Is it the price, or something else?"
>
> **If features:** "Which features are you uncertain about?"
>
> **If decision-making:** "Who else needs to be involved in this decision?"
>
> [Address the real concern, don't just let them go]

### "We're locked into a contract with [Competitor]"

> "I hear that a lot. How much longer on the contract? [Answer]"
>
> **If <6 months:** "Okay, so you could switch in [timeframe]. What if I send you a proposal now, and we schedule a kickoff for when your contract ends?"
>
> **If >6 months:** "Got it. Are you happy with them, or just stuck? [Answer] The reason I ask is many agencies are willing to break contracts if the savings justify it. Want to see if that's the case for you?"

## 🎁 Freemium / Trial Strategy

### Option 1: 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

> "Try Copper AI for 30 days. If you don't save money, we'll refund every penny."

**Pros:** Removes risk, increases conversions  
**Cons:** Requires trust in the product (you must deliver)

### Option 2: Free Pilot (First 5 Caregivers)

> "For the first month, we'll set up 5 of your caregivers for free. If it works, you upgrade to the full plan. If not, no charge."

**Pros:** Lower risk, foot in the door  
**Cons:** Harder to track, may delay revenue

### Option 3: Freemium EVV Only

> "We have a free tier that just does basic EVV compliance. If you want the advanced features (no-show reduction, 24/7 AI, etc.), that's $500/month."

**Pros:** Land-and-expand strategy  
**Cons:** May cannibalize paid customers

**Recommendation:** Start with Option 1 (30-Day MBG). It's the cleanest.

## 💡 Final Pricing Tips

1. **Lead with value, not price** - Always show ROI before discussing cost
2. **Use annual pricing** - Improves cash flow and reduces churn
3. **Discount strategically** - Only for annual, referrals, or pilots
4. **Compare to alternatives** - "Cheaper than mobile EVV apps"
5. **Make it easy** - "Only $27/day" or "$53/caregiver/month"
6. **Offer guarantee** - "30-day money-back guarantee" removes risk
7. **Upsell later** - Don't try to sell everything on Day 1

---

**Remember:** Price is only an objection if they don't see the value. Show the value first!
