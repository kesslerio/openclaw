## 📨 SEQUENCE 3: Comment Strategy (Passive Outreach)

**Goal:** Build visibility before direct outreach

### How It Works:

1. Find your target's posts (check their profile)
2. Leave thoughtful, valuable comments
3. Do this 2-3x before reaching out
4. They recognize your name when you connect

### Comment Templates

**If They Post About Staffing Challenges:**

```

agencies use voice AI to reduce no-shows by 60%, which helps
retain the caregivers you DO have. Happy to share what's
```

```
specifically for home health ops managers facing this. Would
love to hear your take on [specific point from their post].
```

**If They Celebrate a Win:**


## 🎬 BEST PRACTICES

### Do's ✅

- Personalize every message (use their name, company, city)
- Lead with value (ROI, case studies, free resources)
- Keep it short (under 150 words)
- Ask questions (engagement driver)
- Follow up (80% of closes happen after 5+ touches)

### Don'ts ❌

- Generic connection requests ("I'd like to add you to my network")
- Pitch immediately after connecting (build rapport first)
- Copy-paste the same message (people can tell)
- Spam with multiple messages per day
- Give up after one message (persistence wins)
