## 🎯 Targeting Criteria

**Ideal Pilot Agencies:**

- 300-1000 visits/month (mid-size, decision-makers accessible)
- No-show rate >15% (clear pain point)
- Already on ClearCare (integration ready)
- Operations manager active on LinkedIn
- Posted about staffing/scheduling challenges

**Red Flags:**

- Very small (<100 visits) — ROI not compelling enough
- Very large (>2000 visits) — Long sales cycle
- Recent tech changes — Change fatigue
- No-show rate <10% — Less urgent need

## 📝 Discovery Questions

1. "What's your current no-show rate?" (establish baseline)
2. "How do you handle patient confirmations today?" (manual pain)
3. "What % of your caregivers don't have smartphones?" (voice EVV fit)
4. "Who handles schedule changes when a visit falls through?" (ops burden)
5. "What happens when EVV isn't completed?" (compliance pressure)
