## Partnership Models

### Model 1: Technical Integration (API Partnership)

**Structure:**

- Copper AI builds native integration with EVV system API
- EVV system lists Copper AI in their "App Marketplace" or "Integrations" page
- Copper AI handles sales, onboarding, support
- EVV system gets referral fee (10-20%)

**Timeline:** 8-12 weeks to build integration
**Revenue:** Referral fee on every customer using the integration

**Best for:** Large EVV platforms with existing app marketplaces (WellSky, Axxess)

---

### Model 2: White-Label Partnership

**Structure:**

- EVV system licenses Copper AI technology
- Rebrand as "[EVV System] Voice Agent"
- EVV system owns customer relationship
- Copper AI provides backend infrastructure + support

**Timeline:** 12-16 weeks (customization, branding, testing)
**Revenue:** $5,000-10,000/month platform fee + $50-100 per customer per month

**Best for:** Mid-market EVV systems looking to differentiate (Axxess, AlayaCare)

---

### Model 3: Co-Sell Partnership

**Structure:**

- Joint go-to-market strategy
- EVV system's sales team pitches Copper AI alongside their product
- Split revenue 50/50 or 60/40
- Shared customer success responsibility

**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks to align sales teams
**Revenue:** Recurring revenue split

**Best for:** Smaller EVV systems with strong sales teams (KanTime, Rosemark)

---

### Model 4: Acquisition Target

**Structure:**

- EVV system acquires Copper AI outright
- Copper AI becomes their "AI division"
- Full integration into product suite

**Timeline:** 3-6 months due diligence + close
**Revenue:** One-time acquisition price ($2M-10M range, depends on ARR)

**Best for:** Large, well-funded EVV systems (WellSky, Axxess) looking to own AI capability

## Key Objections & Responses

### Objection 1: "We're already building AI features internally."

**Response:**
"That's great! I'd love to hear about your roadmap. What we're finding is that voice AI is complex—speech-to-text, LLM orchestration, telephony integration. It took us 12 months to get it production-ready. If you're 6-12 months away, we could bridge the gap for your customers in the meantime, and you could evaluate a partnership or acquisition down the road."

---

### Objection 2: "Our customers aren't asking for this."

**Response:**
"Fair point. How many of your customers currently use answering services or manual confirmation processes? [Pause for answer.] We've found that agencies don't ask for 'voice AI' specifically, but they complain about no-shows and admin burden constantly. This solves both. Would it make sense to test with 5 of your customers and let them tell us if it's valuable?"

---

### Objection 3: "We don't do revenue shares / partnerships."

**Response:**
"Totally understand. What if we start with a simple API integration and marketplace listing? You don't have to commit to revenue share—just make it easy for your mutual customers to connect the two systems. If it drives value, we can explore deeper partnership later."

---

### Objection 4: "Our product roadmap is locked for 2026."

**Response:**
"No problem. Our integration doesn't require changes to your product—we build to your existing API. The integration lives on our side, and your customers just authenticate via OAuth. It's plug-and-play. We've done this with WellSky and Axxess already."

---

### Objection 5: "What if you get acquired or shut down?"

**Response:**
"Great question. We can structure the partnership with an SLA and redundancy plan. If we're acquired, the acquiring company would honor existing partnerships (it's an asset for them). If we shut down, we'd give 6 months' notice and help migrate customers. That said, we're growing 30% MoM and well-funded, so that's unlikely."
