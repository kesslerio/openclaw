# Copper AI Demo Script

_15-Minute Discovery & Demo_

## Pre-Demo Checklist

**Before the call:**

- [ ] Review prospect's info (agency size, location, EVV system)
- [ ] Customize demo to their specific pain points
- [ ] Have ROI calculator ready with their numbers pre-filled
- [ ] Test screen share and audio
- [ ] Silence notifications

**Materials to have open:**

- Demo environment
- ROI calculator (`marketing/copper-roi-calculator.html`)
- Competitive battlecard (if they mention competitors)
- Pricing sheet
- Case study (similar agency size)

---

## Demo Structure (15 Minutes)

1. **Discovery** (3 min) - Understand their world
2. **The Problem** (2 min) - Validate pain points
3. **The Demo** (7 min) - Show Copper AI in action
4. **The Numbers** (2 min) - ROI calculator
5. **Next Steps** (1 min) - Close for trial/contract

---

## 1. Discovery (3 minutes)

### Opening

```
"Hey [Name], thanks for joining! Before I show you Copper AI,
I want to make sure I show you the parts that matter most to you.

Can I ask a few quick questions about [Agency Name]?"
```

### Key Questions

**About their operation:**

- "How many patients are you serving right now?"
- "How many caregivers on your team?"
- "What EVV system are you using?" [ClearCare/WellSky/Axxess/etc]

**About their pain:**

- "How are you handling shift confirmations today?"
- "What about patient appointment reminders?"
- "How much time does your team spend on the phone each week?"

**About their goals:**

- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would you automate first?"
- "What's driving you to look at voice AI now?"

### Listen For:

✅ High call volume (>200/month)
✅ Manual calling eating up staff time
✅ No-show problems
✅ After-hours coverage gaps
✅ EVV double-entry frustration
✅ Cost concerns with current solution

### Red Flags:

❌ Very low volume (<50 calls/month) - might be too small
❌ Complex custom workflows - might need enterprise
❌ Already have in-house dev team building - might prefer Vapi

## 2. The Problem (2 minutes)

### Frame Their Pain Back to Them

```
"Okay, so let me make sure I understand:

You're doing [X calls per week] manually, which takes [Y hours]
of staff time. That's costing you roughly $[Z] per month in labor,
plus you're missing [%] of shifts due to no-shows.

And right now, you're [their current solution], which costs
$[amount] but doesn't integrate with [EVV system], so there's
double-entry.

Does that sound about right?"
```

### Validate the Cost

```
"Just to put a number on it: at [X] hours per week at $[Y/hour],
that's roughly $[Z] per month in labor costs. Plus the revenue
lost from no-shows.

Would you say that's in the ballpark?"
```

**Goal:** Get them to say "yes" and acknowledge the cost.

## 3. The Demo (7 minutes)

### Introduction (30 seconds)

```
"Great. So Copper AI is specifically built for home health agencies.
Everything I'm about to show you was designed around workflows like
shift confirmations, patient reminders, and caregiver check-ins.

Let me show you how it works."
```

---

### Demo Flow

#### A. Dashboard Overview (1 minute)

**Show:**

- Clean, simple interface
- Today's call queue
- Recent call history
- Real-time status

**Script:**

```
"This is your dashboard. Every morning, you see your call queue
for the day. These are pulled automatically from [their EVV system].

No manual uploads, no spreadsheets. Just synced data."
```

**Point Out:**

- ✅ "Zero technical setup - no IT team needed"
- ✅ "Updates in real-time from your EVV"
- ✅ "Works on phone, tablet, desktop"

---

#### B. Use Case #1: Shift Confirmations (2 minutes)

**Scenario Setup:**

```
"Let's say you have 30 caregivers working tomorrow. Normally,
someone on your team spends 1-2 hours calling each one to confirm.

Here's what Copper does instead."
```

**Demo Steps:**

1. **Show the call list**

   ```
   "Here's tomorrow's schedule, pulled from ClearCare.
   30 caregivers, 30 shifts."
   ```

2. **Click "Start Calls"**

   ```
   "I click one button. Copper starts calling everyone at 8am.

   The AI agent introduces itself, confirms the shift details,
   and asks if they can confirm."
   ```

3. **Play a sample call** (30 seconds)

   ```
   [PLAY RECORDING]

   Agent: "Hi Maria, this is the automated scheduling assistant
          from [Agency]. I'm calling to confirm your shift tomorrow
          at 2pm with Mrs. Johnson on Maple Street. Can you confirm?"

   Caregiver: "Yes, I'll be there."

   Agent: "Perfect! Your shift is confirmed. Thank you!"
   ```

4. **Show the results**

   ```
   "Within 15 minutes, all 30 calls are done. Here's the status:

   - 25 confirmed automatically
   - 3 went to voicemail (flagged for follow-up)
   - 2 said they can't make it (flagged urgent)

   Your EVV system is already updated. Your scheduler sees the
   2 urgent flags and calls to find replacements."
   ```

**Value Prop:**

- ✅ 1-2 hours → 15 minutes
- ✅ Zero errors (no "forgot to call")
- ✅ Automatic EVV sync
- ✅ Flags issues for human follow-up

---

#### C. Use Case #2: Patient Appointment Reminders (2 minutes)

**Scenario Setup:**

```
"Now let's look at patient reminders. You mentioned a [X]% no-show
rate. That's costing you roughly $[Y] per month in lost revenue.

Here's how we fix that."
```

**Demo Steps:**

1. **Show appointment list**

   ```
   "These are your visits for tomorrow. Copper calls each patient
   24 hours before their appointment."
   ```

2. **Play sample patient call** (30 seconds)

   ```
   [PLAY RECORDING]

   Agent: "Hi Mr. Smith, this is a reminder from [Agency] about
          your physical therapy appointment tomorrow at 10am.
          Can I confirm you'll be home?"

   Patient: "Yes, I'll be here."

   Agent: "Great! We'll see you at 10am. Have a wonderful day!"
   ```

3. **Show the smart routing**

   ```
   "If a patient says they need to reschedule, Copper offers them
   3 alternative times based on your caregiver availability.

   If they can't decide, it flags for your scheduler to call back.

   But 80% of confirmations happen automatically."
   ```

**Value Prop:**

- ✅ 30-50% reduction in no-shows
- ✅ $[X]/month in recovered revenue
- ✅ Better patient experience
- ✅ Scheduler focuses on complex cases

## 5. Next Steps (1 minute)

### Trial or Contract

**If they're ready:**

```
"Great! Here's what happens next:

1. We sign a simple agreement (no long-term contract required)
2. Our team sets up your account (3-5 days)
3. We import your data from [EVV system]
4. You test it for 2 weeks with a subset of calls
5. If you love it, we roll it out fully

Does that sound like a plan?"
```

**If they need time:**

```
"No pressure! What would help you make a decision?

Would it help to:
- See a case study from a similar agency?
- Talk to one of our current customers?
- Get a custom proposal with exact pricing?
- Test it with just one workflow first?

What makes sense?"
```

### Calendar Close

**Always leave with a next step:**

```
"Let's book 15 minutes next week to review [whatever they asked for].
Does [Day] at [Time] work?"
```

## Objection Handling Cheat Sheet

### "This seems expensive"

**Response:**

```
"I get it. $[X]/month feels like a lot until you compare it to
[their current solution]:

Current: $[A]/month + [B] hours of labor = $[C] total
Copper: $[X]/month (all-in)
Savings: $[D]/month

You're actually spending less and getting more done."
```

---

### "What if it doesn't work for us?"

**Response:**

```
"Great question. We have a 30-day out clause. If it's not working
after the first month, you can cancel. No penalty.

But here's what typically happens: agencies find 1-2 workflows
where it works great, then expand from there.

We can start small - just shift confirmations - and add more as
you see results."
```

---

```
"What went wrong with [Competitor]?"

[Competitor]: [Their weakness]

We're built specifically for home health, not generic voice AI.
The workflows you need are already built."
```

```
Is it:
- Budget approval?
```

## Demo Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ DO:

- **Ask questions first** - Understand their world before showing features
- **Use their numbers** - Real ROI, not generic examples
- **Show, don't tell** - Play recordings, show results
- **Acknowledge competition** - If they mention Vapi/Retell, address it honestly
- **Focus on outcomes** - Time saved, money saved, problems solved
- **Keep it simple** - 15 minutes max, don't overwhelm
- **End with next step** - Always book the next call

### ❌ DON'T:

- **Feature dump** - Nobody cares about your tech stack
- **Skip discovery** - You can't demo well without understanding their pain
- **Bash competitors** - Acknowledge strengths, highlight differences
- **Oversell** - If they're a bad fit, say so
- **Rush** - Let them ask questions
- **Leave without next step** - "Think about it" = dead deal

## Special Scenarios

### Scenario: They're using Vapi/Retell already

**Ask:**

```
"How's that working out? What made you look at alternatives?"
```

**Listen for:**

- Hidden costs piling up
- Developer dependency
- Complexity/maintenance burden
- Lack of home health features

**Position:**

```
"Vapi/Retell is a great platform if you have a dev team and want
maximum control. But for most home health agencies, it's overkill.

Copper gives you 80% of the flexibility at 20% of the cost and
complexity. Plus we understand your workflows out of the box."
```

---

### Scenario: They have <100 calls/month

**Honest assessment:**

```
"Based on [X] calls/month, you're probably not quite ready for
Copper yet. Our sweet spot is 200+ calls.

At your volume, manual calling or [cheaper alternative] might
make more sense.

But when you hit 150-200 calls/month, let's revisit. That's
when the ROI really kicks in."
```

**Why be honest:** Builds trust for future. They'll remember you didn't oversell.

---

### Scenario: They want to build it themselves

**Ask:**

```
"That's ambitious! What's driving the build vs buy decision?"
```

**If cost:**

```
"Building will cost $20-50K in dev time, plus $5-10K/month
to maintain. Copper is $[X]/month with zero dev work.

But if you have the budget and team, building might give you
exactly what you want."
```

**If control:**

```
"I get that. Copper might not be right if you need that level
of control. But I'd encourage you to prototype with us first.

Test with real calls for 30 days. If it works, you save the
build cost. If not, you learned what you need to build."
```
