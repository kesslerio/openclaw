# Copper AI - Sales Objections Handbook

_Every objection you'll hear and how to handle it_

---

## How to Use This Handbook

**Before every call:** Review the most common objections for your prospect type

**During objection:**

1. Listen fully (don't interrupt)
2. Acknowledge their concern
3. Use the response framework below
4. Ask a question to move forward

**Never:** Get defensive, argue, or dismiss their concern

---

## The 10 Most Common Objections

### 1. "It's too expensive"

**What they're really saying:** "I don't see the value yet" or "I need to justify this to someone"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"I understand cost is always a concern, especially when evaluating new tools."

**Reframe:**
"Let me ask: what's your current no-show rate costing you each month?"

[Let them calculate or help them: 20% no-shows × 100 shifts × $120/shift = $2,400/month lost]

"So you're losing $2,400/month to no-shows alone. Copper AI is $497/month and reduces that by 60%, saving you $1,440/month in recovered revenue. Plus 15 hours/week in staff time worth $1,500/month. Total value: $2,940/month for a $497 investment."

**Close:**
"Does that ROI make sense? The question isn't whether you can afford it—it's whether you can afford NOT to have it."

**Alternative responses:**

**vs Cheaper solutions (Dialora $197):**
"Dialora is cheaper upfront, but you'll spend 5-10 hours/week manually syncing to your EVV. At $25/hour, that's $500-1,000/month in labor. Our EVV integration makes us cheaper overall, plus you get vertical-specific features they don't have."

**vs DIY (Vapi/Retell):**
"You could build it yourself with Vapi for $0.05/min advertised. But actual cost is $0.18-0.33/min once you add all required services, plus 40-60 hours of dev time at $100/hr. That's $4,000-6,000 upfront and $1,000+/month ongoing. We're turnkey for $497."

### 2. "We already have a solution that works"

**What they're really saying:** "Change is risky" or "We're comfortable with the status quo"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"That's great that you have something in place. What solution are you currently using?"

[Listen for: answering service, manual process, other software]

**Probe:**
"And how's that working for you? What's your current no-show rate? How many hours per week does your team spend on confirmations?"

[Most will admit it's not perfect]

**Position:**
"Here's what I'm hearing: [their solution] handles [X], but you're still dealing with [pain point]. Copper AI wasn't built to replace [their solution]—it was built to solve the gaps. The fact that you [still have no-shows / still spend hours on calls] tells me there's room for improvement."

**Close:**
"What if we did a 30-day pilot alongside your current solution? You'll see the difference firsthand. If it doesn't improve things, full refund. No risk."

---

### 3. "I need to think about it"

**What they're really saying:** "I'm not convinced yet" or "I need to talk to someone" or "I'm avoiding saying no"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"Of course. This is an important decision."

**Diagnose:**
"Just so I can help you think this through—what specific concerns do you have?"

[Listen for the real objection: budget, timing, tech worries, need approval]

**If Budget:**
"Is it the pricing specifically, or is it justifying the ROI?"
[Then use objection #1 response]

**If Timing:**
"What would need to change for this to be the right time?"
[Often reveals they need a push or have a deadline]

**If Approval:**
"Who else needs to be involved in this decision? Let's get them on a call so I can answer their questions directly."

**If Avoiding No:**
"I appreciate you being thoughtful about this. Can I ask—on a scale of 1-10, how interested are you in solving [their pain point]?"

[If 7+]: "Great! So it sounds like you see the value. What's holding you back from moving forward today?"
[If <7]: "I understand this might not be the right fit. Can I ask what would make it a 9 or 10 for you?"

**Close:**
"How about this: let's schedule a follow-up call for [specific date]. I'll send you [case study/ROI calc/reference customer contact] in the meantime. Sound good?"

### 4. "We don't have the budget right now"

**What they're really saying:** "This isn't a priority" or "We have other expenses" or "It's not in this year's budget"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"I totally understand budget constraints."

**Reframe:**
"Let me ask: if Copper AI could pay for itself in the first month, would budget still be an issue?"

[Walk through ROI calculation showing it pays for itself]

**Alternative:**
"What if we started small? Our Starter tier is $297/month—less than what most agencies pay for a single shift's no-show. You could pilot it for 30 days, and if you don't see at least $297 in value, full refund."

**Payment terms:**
"We also offer flexible payment terms. Some customers prefer to budget it as part of their EVV/software stack rather than a new line item. Does that help?"

**Prioritization:**
"I hear you on budget. But let me ask: how much are no-shows costing you per month? [$2,000-3,000] So you're currently losing $2-3K/month to a problem that costs $497/month to solve. The question is: can you afford NOT to fix this?"

### 5. "How do I know it will actually work?"

**What they're really saying:** "I'm skeptical" or "I need proof" or "I've been burned before"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"That's a fair question. You should be skeptical—there are a lot of solutions that overpromise and underdeliver."

**Provide Proof:**
"Here's what we've seen with 100+ home health agencies:

- Average no-show reduction: 60% (from 20% to 8%)
- Average time saved: 15 hours/week
- Setup time: 7-10 days
- Customer satisfaction: 4.9/5 stars"

**Social Proof:**
"Let me send you a case study from [similar agency]. They had the same concern. After 30 days, their no-shows dropped from 18% to 7%. I can also connect you with [reference customer name] who can speak to their experience."

**Guarantee:**
"But here's what really matters: we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you don't see improvement in the first month—better confirmation rates, fewer no-shows, time savings—just tell us and we'll refund you. You're not betting on whether it works. We are."

**Close:**
"So the real question isn't 'will it work?'—we've proven it works. The question is: are you willing to try it risk-free for 30 days?"

### 6. "Our caregivers won't like talking to an AI"

**What they're really saying:** "Change management is hard" or "My team resists new tech"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"I hear that concern a lot, and it's valid. Change can be challenging."

**Reframe:**
"But here's what's interesting: we've found that caregivers actually PREFER the AI calls. Why? Because the AI is:

- Consistent (same voice, same script, every time)
- Available 24/7 (they can call back anytime)
- Never rushed or distracted
- Never forgets to call

Compare that to a human caller who might be having a bad day, forget to call, or call at inconvenient times."

**Real Data:**
"In our customer surveys, 87% of caregivers said they liked the AI confirmations better than manual calls. The consistency matters more than the human voice."

**Pilot Approach:**
"Here's what I recommend: pilot it with 5-10 of your caregivers for 2 weeks. Get their feedback. If they hate it, we can adjust the approach or discontinue. But I'm confident they'll appreciate the reliability."

**Human Escalation:**
"And remember: the AI handles routine confirmations. If someone needs to reschedule, has a question, or wants to talk to a human, the AI escalates to your team immediately. It's not replacing humans—it's handling the repetitive work so humans can focus on complex situations."

### 7. "What if the AI makes a mistake?"

**What they're really saying:** "I'm worried about liability" or "I need control"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"That's an important concern, especially in healthcare where mistakes have consequences."

**Explain Safeguards:**
"Here's how we mitigate that:

1. **Human oversight**: You review and approve all call scripts before they go live. You control exactly what the AI says.

2. **Escalation**: If the AI encounters anything unexpected (caregiver says they quit, emergency, complex schedule change), it immediately escalates to a human. It doesn't try to handle what it can't.

3. **Audit trail**: Every call is logged with timestamp, recording, and outcome. You can review any call anytime.

4. **Fallback**: If there's ever a technical issue, confirmations can revert to manual process while we fix it.

5. **HIPAA compliance**: All calls are encrypted, secure, and compliant. We've been audited and approved for healthcare use."

**Reality Check:**
"Compare that to human callers who might mishear something, forget to log a call, or make a typo in your EVV. The AI is actually MORE reliable because it:

- Never forgets
- Never makes transcription errors
- Always logs everything
- Follows the script exactly"

**Close:**
"You maintain full control. The AI is a tool that executes your process perfectly, every time. And with our 30-day guarantee, you can test it risk-free."

### 8. "We need to integrate with [obscure EVV system]"

**What they're really saying:** "We have unique needs" or "I'm worried about compatibility"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"Great question. Integration is critical for this to work smoothly."

**Assess:**
"Which EVV system are you using?"

**If Supported (WellSky, Axxess, ClearCare, Sandata):**
"Perfect! We have native integration with [their system]. Setup takes 7-10 days. We pull shifts, push confirmations, all automatic. We've done this with [X] other [EVV name] customers."

**If Not Supported:**
"[EVV name]—I haven't heard of that one. Let me check with our team, but we have three options:

1. **API integration** - If they have an API, we can build a custom integration (2-4 weeks, may have additional cost)
2. **CSV sync** - We can do automated file uploads/downloads (hourly or daily sync)
3. **Manual entry** - As a fallback, you can manually trigger calls or enter confirmations

Most EVV systems have APIs nowadays. Let me confirm what's possible and get back to you within 24 hours. Does that work?"

**Positioning:**
"The good news is that even without perfect integration, Copper AI still saves you time on calling. Integration just makes it seamless. But we're flexible and can work with most systems."

### 9. "What happens if your company goes out of business?"

**What they're really saying:** "You're a startup, and I'm worried about continuity"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"That's a smart question. Vendor reliability matters."

**Reassure:**
"Here's what you should know:

1. **Financial stability**: We're profitable and growing 30% month-over-month. We're not burning VC cash hoping for an exit.

2. **Customer base**: 100+ agencies trust us with their daily operations. We're not going anywhere.

3. **Contract terms**: We don't lock you into long-term contracts. If we ever did go away (we won't), you can switch solutions with 30 days' notice.

4. **Data ownership**: All your data—call logs, recordings, confirmations—is yours. You can export it anytime.

5. **Open communication**: If we were ever acquired or shutting down, we'd give customers 6 months' notice and help migrate to alternatives."

**Reframe:**
"But let's be honest: the bigger risk is continuing with your current solution. You're losing $2-3K/month to no-shows RIGHT NOW. That's real money gone. Copper AI is the solution that stops that bleeding. Even if we magically disappeared tomorrow (we won't), you'd have saved thousands in the meantime."

**Close:**
"Month-to-month contract. 30-day guarantee. No long-term risk. The only risk is staying where you are."

### 10. "Let me talk to my team / partner / spouse first"

**What they're really saying:** "I'm not the sole decision-maker" or "I want buy-in" or "I'm not ready to decide"

**Response Framework:**

**Acknowledge:**
"Absolutely. This should be a team decision."

**Diagnose:**
"Just to make sure I can help: who else is involved in this decision?"

[Listen for: partner, co-owner, operations manager, finance person]

**Facilitate:**
"Here's what I'd suggest: let's schedule a follow-up call with [decision-maker]. I can walk them through the same demo and answer their questions directly. That way everyone's on the same page. Does [date/time] work?"

**Provide Tools:**
"In the meantime, I'll send you:

- ROI calculator (so you can show them the numbers)
- Case study from a similar agency
- One-pager with key benefits
- Demo recording from today's call

That should give them everything they need to evaluate it. Sound good?"

**Alternative (if they resist):**
"I understand if you want to discuss internally first. What specific concerns or questions do you think they'll have?"

[Address those proactively]

**Close:**
"When do you think you'll have a chance to discuss it? I'd love to follow up after you've talked and answer any questions that come up."

## Objection Patterns by Prospect Type

### Small Agency Objections:

**Top 3:**

1. "Too expensive" (budget is tight)
2. "We're too small" (don't think they need it)
3. "Too complicated" (afraid of tech)

**Strategy:**

- Focus on time savings (owner is probably doing everything)
- Emphasize ease of setup (no tech skills needed)
- Start with smallest tier ($297)
- 30-day guarantee = no risk

---

### Medium Agency Objections:

**Top 3:**

1. "We already have a solution" (answering service or manual process)
2. "Need to think about it" (need approval from partners)
3. "Integration concerns" (have EVV they love)

**Strategy:**

- Show how it complements existing tools
- Get multiple decision-makers on call
- Emphasize EVV integration (it enhances their current stack)
- Pilot approach (test alongside current solution)

---

### Large Agency Objections:

**Top 3:**

1. "Need vendor approval" (procurement process)
2. "Security/compliance questions" (HIPAA, SOC 2, etc.)
3. "Contract terms" (want annual, SLA, etc.)

**Strategy:**

- Provide security documentation upfront
- Offer to speak with IT/compliance team
- Flexible contract terms (can do annual with discount)
- Position as enterprise-ready (SLA, dedicated support, etc.)

## Advanced Objection Handling Techniques

### The "Feel, Felt, Found" Method

**When they object, use this framework:**

"I understand how you **feel**. Many of our customers **felt** the same way before trying Copper AI. Here's what they **found**..."

**Example:**

- Feel: "I understand how you feel about the cost."
- Felt: "Many agency owners felt the same way—$497/month seemed like a lot."
- Found: "But what they found was that they saved $2,000+/month in no-shows and staff time, making it a no-brainer investment."

---

### The Boomerang

**When they object, turn it into a reason to buy:**

**Objection:** "We're too busy to implement something new right now."

**Boomerang:** "That's exactly WHY you need this. If you're too busy to set up an automation tool that saves 15 hours/week, you're going to stay buried in busy-work forever. Let us handle the setup—it takes 10 days, and then you get your time back."

---

### The Takeaway

**When price is the sticking point, remove the option:**

**Objection:** "That's still too expensive."

**Takeaway:** "I understand. It sounds like Copper AI might not be the right fit for your budget right now. Let me ask though: what's Plan B? How are you going to reduce no-shows and save staff time without automation? Because the problem isn't going away."

[Often they'll rethink when they realize there's no good alternative]

## Price Objection Deep-Dive

### Pricing Psychology

**Never apologize for your price. Instead:**

1. **Anchor to value, not cost**
   - Bad: "It's only $497/month"
   - Good: "It saves most agencies $2,000-3,000/month"

2. **Break it down**
   - "That's $16/day. Less than the cost of a single no-show."

3. **Compare to alternatives**
   - "Compared to an answering service at $2,500/month, this is an $24,000/year savings."

4. **Show ROI timeline**
   - "Most agencies see positive ROI within 2-3 weeks."

---

### "Too Expensive" Variants

**"I can't afford that right now"**
→ "I understand. What CAN you afford to keep losing to no-shows every month? Because that's the real cost."

**"That's more than I budgeted"**
→ "Where is the budget currently going? Often agencies find they can reallocate from [answering service / overtime pay / other tools] to Copper AI and actually save money."

**"I need to check with my accountant"**
→ "Great idea. Here's our ROI calculator. Show them that for every $1 spent on Copper AI, you save $4-6. What accountant would say no to that?"

**"Maybe next quarter when we have more budget"**
→ "I understand waiting, but let me ask: how much will no-shows cost you between now and next quarter? [Do math: 3 months × $2K/month = $6K lost.] By waiting, you're choosing to lose $6K to save $1.5K. Does that make sense?"

## Closing Framework

### After Handling Objection:

**1. Confirm resolution:**
"Does that address your concern about [objection]?"

**2. Trial close:**
"Based on everything we've discussed, does Copper AI sound like it would solve [their pain point]?"

**3. Next step:**
"Great. So what I'm hearing is you're ready to move forward. Let's get you set up. I'll send the contract today, and we can have you live within 10 days. Sound good?"

**4. If they hesitate:**
"What else is holding you back?"
[Uncover the real objection and address it]

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Don't argue:** Never say "You're wrong" or "That's not true"
❌ **Don't interrupt:** Let them finish their objection completely
❌ **Don't get defensive:** Stay calm and curious
❌ **Don't discount immediately:** Lowering price = lowering perceived value
❌ **Don't bad-mouth competitors:** Position yourself, don't trash others
❌ **Don't overwhelm with features:** Focus on their specific pain point
❌ **Don't lie or overpromise:** Honesty builds trust
