## Optimization Schedule

### Week 1-2: Learning Phase

- Let algorithms gather data
- Don't make major changes
- Monitor for broken links, form issues

### Week 3-4: Initial Optimization

- Pause underperforming keywords (CTR <2%)
- Increase bids on high-converting keywords
- Test new ad copy variations

### Month 2: Scaling

- Increase budget on best-performing campaigns by 20%
- Add negative keywords
- Expand into related keywords

### Month 3+: Mature

- Target CPA bidding (once 50+ conversions)
- Automated bidding strategies
- Audience expansion
