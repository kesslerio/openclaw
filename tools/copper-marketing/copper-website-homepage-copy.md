### Headline

Automate Shift Confirmations. Reduce No-Shows. Get Your Evenings Back.

### Three-Step Process

**Step 1: We Pull Tomorrow's Schedule**
Icon: 📅
Copper AI connects to your EVV system (WellSky, Axxess, ClearCare, Sandata) and automatically pulls the next day's shifts every evening.

**Step 2: AI Calls Your Caregivers**
Icon: 📞
Our AI voice agent calls each caregiver 24 hours before their shift to confirm. Natural conversation. Consistent every time.

**Step 3: Results Sync Back Automatically**
Icon: ✅
Confirmed shifts update in your EVV in real-time. Your coordinators get alerts only when someone can't make it. Zero manual data entry.

## Conversion Optimization Tips

### Above the Fold (Critical)

1. Clear value proposition (reduce no-shows by 60%)
2. Visual proof (video or screenshot)
3. Single, prominent CTA (Book a Demo)
4. Trust badges (HIPAA, EVV logos)

### Social Proof

- Customer logos (with permission)
- Testimonials with photos
- Specific numbers (60% reduction, 15 hrs saved)
- Video testimonials (if available)

### Remove Friction

- No pricing hidden behind "Contact Sales"
- Clear next steps ("Book a 15-minute demo")
- Money-back guarantee
- Fast setup time (7-10 days, not weeks)

### Mobile Optimization

- Sticky CTA button on mobile
- Click-to-call phone numbers
- Simplified navigation
- Fast load times (<3 seconds)
