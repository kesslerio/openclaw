## 💡 Tips for Success

### Sales:

- **Personalize everything** - Templates are starting points, not copy-paste
- **Track metrics religiously** - What's measured improves
- **Iterate fast** - If something's not working after 10 tries, change it
- **Lead with value** - Not features, not "our product," but their outcome

### Marketing:

- **Be consistent** - Better to post 3x/week consistently than 10x one week
- **Engage, don't broadcast** - Comment on others' content more than you post
- **Test everything** - A/B test subject lines, posting times, content types
- **Use data** - Track what gets engagement, do more of that

### Executive:

- **Empower the team** - These templates remove roadblocks, let them run
- **Set clear metrics** - Use the benchmarks in the playbooks
- **Review monthly** - What's working? What's not? Adjust.
- **Celebrate wins** - When templates lead to closed deals, share that
