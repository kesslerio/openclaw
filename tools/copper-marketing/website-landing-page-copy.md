## Conversion Optimization Notes

### Above the Fold

- Clear value prop: "Stop wasting 15 hours/week"
- Specific numbers (not vague "save time")
- Two CTAs: Calculator (low commitment) + Demo (high intent)
- Social proof immediately visible

### Throughout Page

- Use real numbers ($18K saved, 40% fewer no-shows)
- Real customer names and companies (build trust)
- Visual proof (screenshots, videos, call recordings)
- Multiple CTAs (every section can lead to conversion)

### Objection Handling

- FAQ addresses every common concern
- Comparison table handles "why not X?" questions
- Testimonials prove it works for real agencies
- Free trial removes risk

### Trust Signals

- HIPAA compliance prominent
- Customer logos/testimonials
- Specific results (not "save time" but "6.5 hours/week")
- Money-back guarantee / easy cancellation
