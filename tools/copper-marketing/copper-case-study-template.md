## Template Structure

Every great case study follows the same narrative arc:

1. **Meet the customer** (who they are, what they do)
2. **The problem** (pain points, challenges, context)
3. **The solution** (how they use Copper AI)
4. **The results** (metrics, quotes, impact)
5. **The future** (what's next, lessons learned)

## Interview Questions (For Collecting Case Study Info)

### Before Copper AI:

1. What was your biggest operational challenge?
2. How much time did your team spend on [task]?
3. What was your no-show rate?
4. What solutions did you try before Copper AI?
5. What was the "breaking point" that made you look for a solution?

### Decision Process:

6. What made you choose Copper AI over alternatives?
7. What were your concerns before implementing?
8. How long did implementation take?
9. What surprised you about the setup process?

### After Implementation:

10. What metrics have improved since going live?
11. What's the time/cost savings you've seen?
12. What unexpected benefits did you discover?
13. How has your team responded?
14. How have your caregivers/patients responded?

### Looking Forward:

15. What are you planning to automate next?
16. Would you recommend Copper AI to other agencies? Why?
17. What advice would you give to agencies considering automation?

## Example: Real Case Study Outline

**Customer:** iCare Home Health (Colorado)

**Challenge:**

- 20% no-show rate costing $15k/month
- Office manager spending 15 hrs/week on confirmations
- Answering service ($2,500/mo) unreliable

**Solution:**

- Implemented Copper AI in October 2025
- Integrated with ClearCare EVV
- Automated shift confirmations + patient check-ins

**Results:**

- No-shows: 20% → 8% (60% reduction)
- Time saved: 15 hrs/week → 2 hrs/week
- Cost savings: $2,500/mo (answering service) → $497/mo (Copper AI)
- Net savings: $2,003/month ($24k/year)

**Testimonial:**

> "I was worried this would slow me down or take control away. It did the opposite. I tap to confirm, make small adjustments, and move on. I get my evenings back without cutting corners."
> — Chloe, Director of Nursing

**What's next:**

- Expanding to referral intake automation
- Adding Spanish-language support for bilingual caregivers
