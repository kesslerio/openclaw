# LinkedIn Outreach Playbook for Copper AI

_B2B Prospecting Strategy for Home Health Agencies_

## Strategy Overview

**Target Audience:**

- Home health agency owners
- Operations managers
- COOs/VPs of Operations
- Directors of Nursing
- Agency administrators

**Approach:**

- Value-first (not salesy)
- Industry-specific insights
- Build relationships before pitching
- Multi-touch sequence (5-7 touchpoints)

**Goal:**

- 20-30% connection acceptance rate
- 10-15% response rate
- 3-5% demo booking rate

## Phase 2: Prospecting & Research

### Finding the Right Prospects

**LinkedIn Search Filters:**

1. **Title search:**
   - "Home health owner"
   - "Home care owner"
   - "Home health agency"
   - "Operations manager home health"
   - "Director of nursing home health"

2. **Location:**
   - Target states with most agencies: TX, CA, FL, NY, PA, OH
   - Or focus locally first

3. **Company size:**
   - 11-50 employees (sweet spot)
   - 51-200 employees (good)
   - 1-10 employees (might be too small)

4. **Recent activity:**
   - Posted in last 30 days (more likely to respond)

### Pre-Outreach Research (2-3 minutes per prospect)

**Check:**

- [ ] Recent posts (what are they talking about?)
- [ ] Company website (what services, what EVV system?)
- [ ] Mutual connections (can anyone introduce you?)
- [ ] News/press releases (are they growing/hiring?)
- [ ] Pain signals (job postings for schedulers = high volume)

**Red flags (skip these):**

- Recently sold company
- Less than 1 year in business
- Very small (<5 employees)
- Already using competitor (unless unhappy)

## Phase 3: Connection Request

### Template 1: Industry Insight Hook

**When to use:** They recently posted about operations/staffing

```
Hi [First Name],

Saw your post about [specific topic they mentioned]. The challenge of
[pain point] is something I hear from a lot of home health agencies.

I work with agencies in [their state] on operational efficiency—
specifically reducing the 10-15 hours/week most teams spend on routine
confirmation calls.

Would love to connect and share what's working for similar agencies.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:** Shows you actually read their content, specific to their problem.

---

### Template 2: Mutual Interest Hook

**When to use:** You have something relevant in common

```
Hi [First Name],

I see we're both in the [location] home health community. I specialize
in helping agencies like [Company Name] automate operational workflows—
specifically the time-consuming shift confirmations and patient reminders.

Figured it'd be valuable to connect with others in the space.

Looking forward to it!

[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:** Local connection, specific value prop, no immediate ask.

---

### Template 3: Value-First Hook

**When to use:** No obvious connection point

```
Hi [First Name],

Quick question: Does [Company Name] struggle with the time your team
spends on routine confirmation calls?

I work with home health agencies to automate that work—typically saving
10-15 hours/week and reducing no-shows by 30-50%.

If that's relevant, let's connect. If not, no worries!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:** Direct value prop, low-pressure, easy yes/no.

---

### Template 4: Referral Hook

**When to use:** You worked with a similar agency they might know

```
Hi [First Name],

I recently helped [Similar Agency] in [Location] cut their operational
costs by $18K/year by automating routine calls.

Thought it might be relevant for [Company Name] too—happy to share what
we did if you're interested.

Worth connecting?

[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:** Social proof, specific result, local relevance.

## Connection Request Best Practices

### DO:

✅ **Personalize** - Mention something specific about them
✅ **Keep it short** - 300 characters max (LinkedIn limit)
✅ **Lead with value** - What's in it for them?
✅ **Be specific** - "Automate calls" not "improve operations"
✅ **No pitch** - Just request to connect, not sell
✅ **Sound human** - Conversational, not corporate

### DON'T:

❌ **Generic** - "I'd love to connect" (why?)
❌ **Salesy** - "I have a product perfect for you"
❌ **Long** - Save the details for after they accept
❌ **Lie** - Don't claim mutual connections if there aren't any
❌ **Spam** - Don't send 50 identical requests/day

## Phase 4: Post-Connection Sequence

### Day 0: They Accept (Within 1 Hour)

**Don't pitch immediately!**

**Message:**

```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name]!

Out of curiosity, what's your biggest operational challenge at
[Company Name] right now?

I work with a lot of home health agencies and always curious what's
top of mind for folks.

[Your Name]
```

**Goal:** Start a conversation, understand their world.

---

### Day 3: First Value Touch (If They Responded)

**If they mentioned a specific problem:**

```
Great to hear from you!

The [problem they mentioned] is something we've solved for a few agencies
in [their area]. [Agency Name] had the same issue and we helped them
[specific result].

Would it be helpful if I shared the approach they used? No sales pitch,
just a 5-minute breakdown of what worked.

Let me know!

[Your Name]
```

**If they didn't respond to Day 0:**

```
Hey [First Name],

Not sure if my last message got buried, but wanted to share something
I thought you'd find useful:

[Link to case study / ROI calculator / industry insight]

[Brief context on why it's relevant to them]

Hope it helps!

[Your Name]
```

---

### Day 7: Second Value Touch

**If conversation is going well:**

```
[First Name],

Based on what you shared about [their situation], I think a quick
15-minute call might be valuable.

I can show you exactly how we helped [similar agency] with [their problem],
and see if something similar would work for [Company Name].

Does [Day] at [Time] work? Or here's my calendar: [link]

[Your Name]
```

**If still no response:**

```
[First Name],

I know you're busy, so I'll keep this quick.

I put together a 2-minute ROI calculator that shows exactly what
automating routine calls could save [Company Name]:

[Calculator link]

Just plug in your numbers. No email required.

If the math looks interesting, let's talk. If not, no worries!

[Your Name]
```

---

### Day 14: Third Value Touch

**If they've engaged but haven't booked:**

```

Quick thought: I recorded a 3-minute walkthrough of how [Similar Agency]
automated their shift confirmations.

Figured it might be easier to SEE it than read about it:

[Loom video link]

Worth 3 minutes?

[Your Name]
```

**If still no engagement:**

```

Before I close your file, wanted to leave you with two resources:

1. ROI Calculator: [link]
2. Case Study (similar to your agency): [link]

If things change, I'm here. If not, best of luck with [their business]!

[Your Name]
```

**Before reaching out, engage with their content 3-5 times:**

1. Like their last 2-3 posts
2. Leave a thoughtful comment on one post
3. Share one of their posts with your take

**Comment template:**

```

worked well is [insight/solution].

```

**Why it works:** You're on their radar before you reach out, you look engaged not salesy.

## Scaling Your Outreach

### Daily Limits (To Avoid LinkedIn Restrictions)

**Connection requests:**

- New accounts: 20-30/day
- Established accounts: 50-80/day
- Sales Navigator: 100/day

**Messages:**

- 50-100/day total
- Mix of new conversations and follow-ups

**Profile views:**

- No official limit, but keep it natural

**WARNING SIGNS:**

- "You've reached the weekly limit" message
- Connection requests getting rejected frequently
- Account restrictions/warnings

**If you get restricted:**

- Slow down for 1-2 weeks
- Focus on engaging with content instead
- Increase personalization (less volume, more quality)

## Response Handling

### Common Responses & How to Handle

**"We're not interested right now"**

```
No problem at all, [First Name]!

If timing changes or if you'd just like to stay in touch on industry
trends, I post content about home health operations pretty regularly.

Best of luck with [Company Name]!

[Your Name]
```

---

**"How much does it cost?"**

```
Great question! Pricing depends on call volume.

For an agency your size ([estimated based on research]), we're typically
talking $[X-Y]/month all-in.

Most agencies see ROI in the first month from time savings alone
(before even counting the no-show reduction).

Want to see the exact numbers for [Company Name]? I can walk you through
it in 15 minutes.

[Your Name]
```

---

**"We're already using [Competitor]"**

```
Oh nice! How's that working out for you?

[Wait for response]

I'm always curious what agencies like/dislike about different platforms.
Mind if I ask what made you choose [Competitor]?

[If they're happy, wish them well. If they're frustrated, position Copper as alternative]
```

---

**"Can you send me more information?"**

```
Absolutely! Here are the three things I find most helpful:

1. ROI Calculator (2 minutes, plug in your numbers): [link]
2. Case Study (similar agency, 5-minute read): [link]
3. Quick Demo Video (3 minutes, shows how it works): [link]

After you check those out, let me know if a quick call makes sense
to see if it fits [Company Name].

Sound good?

[Your Name]
```

## Do's and Don'ts Summary

### ✅ DO:

- Personalize every message
- Lead with value, not features
- Engage with their content before reaching out
- Be patient (5-7 touchpoints over weeks)
- Sound human, not corporate
- Respect "not interested"
- Track everything in a spreadsheet/CRM

### ❌ DON'T:

- Send generic "I'd love to connect" requests
- Pitch immediately after connection
- Auto-spam with bots
- Ignore their content/posts
- Give up after one message
- Be pushy or aggressive
- Exceed LinkedIn's daily limits
