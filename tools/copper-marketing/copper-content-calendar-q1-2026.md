## Email Marketing Calendar

### Weekly Newsletter (Every Tuesday)

**Format:**

- 1 industry insight
- 1 Copper AI update/tip
- 1 customer story (abbreviated)
- 1 CTA (demo booking, case study download, etc.)

**Subject Line Rotation:**

- Week 1: Stat/number-focused ("60% fewer no-shows: Here's how")
- Week 2: Question-based ("Are you losing $20k/month to no-shows?")
- Week 3: Customer-focused ("[Customer Name] saved 15 hrs/week")
- Week 4: Actionable tip ("3 ways to reduce no-shows this week")
