## Key Messaging (for all versions)

**DO say:**

- "Purpose-built for home health" (not generic)
- "All-inclusive pricing" (vs hidden fees)
- "No developer required" (vs complex platforms)
- "50-70% cost savings" (vs competitors)
- "60% fewer no-shows" (customer results)
- "15 hours per week saved" (time savings)
- "HIPAA-compliant" (security/compliance)

**DON'T say:**

- "Revolutionary" (overused)
- "Game-changing" (unless from customer quote)
- "First ever" (probably not true)
- "Groundbreaking" (cliché)
- "Disruptive" (buzzword)

**Tone:**

- Confident but not boastful
- Results-focused
- Customer success over feature lists
- Empathy for the industry's challenges
