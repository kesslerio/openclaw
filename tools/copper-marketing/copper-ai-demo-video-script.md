## Music & Sound Design

**Background Music:**

- **Style:** Uplifting, modern, professional (not cheesy corporate)
- **Mood:** Hopeful, efficient, human
- **Sources:**
  - Epidemic Sound ($15/month)
  - Artlist ($20/month)
  - Free: YouTube Audio Library (search "uplifting corporate")

**Sound Effects:**

- Phone ringing (subtle)
- Notification "ding" when tasks complete
- Gentle whoosh transitions
- Keyboard typing (subtle background)

**Mixing:**

- Music at -18dB to -22dB (background, never overpowering voice)
- Voiceover at 0dB (front and center)
- Sound effects at -12dB to -15dB (noticeable but not jarring)

## Export Specs

### YouTube/Website (Primary)

- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080)
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Codec:** H.264
- **Bitrate:** 8-12 Mbps
- **Format:** MP4

### LinkedIn (Native Upload)

- **Same as YouTube**
- **Subtitle file:** SRT (auto-generate in YouTube, download, upload to LinkedIn)
- **First 3 seconds:** Must hook (silent autoplay)

### Instagram/Facebook

- **Resolution:** 1080x1080 (square) or 1080x1920 (vertical)
- **Duration:** 60 seconds max for organic, 30 sec for ads
- **Subtitles:** Burned into video (required for silent watching)

### Twitter/X

- **Resolution:** 1280x720 or 1920x1080
- **Duration:** 2:20 max
- **File size:** Under 512MB
