## ✅ Our Solution: Voice-First EVV

**Copper AI automates EVV compliance through simple phone calls.**

### How It Works:

**1. Pre-Visit Reminder (15 minutes before)**

> AI: "Hi Maria, you have a 2 PM visit with Mr. Johnson. Are you still available?"

**2. Clock-In (Caregiver arrives)**

> AI: "Hi Maria, have you arrived at Mr. Johnson's home?"  
> Maria: "Yes, I just got here."  
> AI: "Great! I'm logging your arrival at 2:05 PM."

**3. Clock-Out (Visit complete)**

> AI: "Hi Maria, have you finished the visit?"  
> Maria: "Yes, just done."  
> AI: "Perfect! Logged at 4:15 PM. Your visit is verified and ready for billing."

### **That's it. No app. No training. 100% compliant.**
