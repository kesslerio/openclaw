## Getting Started

### How long does it take to get set up?

**Short answer:** 3-5 business days from signup to first calls.

**Detailed timeline:**

- **Day 1:** You sign up, we kick off onboarding call

## Technical & Integration

### Which EVV systems do you integrate with?

**Currently supported:**

- ClearCare
- WellSky (formerly ClearCare by WellSky)
- Axxess
- AlayaCare
- Homecare Homebase
- Santrax
- Kantime

**Coming soon:**

- Caretap
- KanTime Healthcare
- Matrix Care

**Don't see yours?** Let us know! We add new integrations based on customer demand.

---

### How does the EVV integration actually work?

**Two-way sync:**

**From EVV to Copper (Outbound calls):**

1. Copper pulls your schedule daily (or real-time)
2. Identifies which shifts/visits need confirmation
3. Generates call list automatically
4. Makes calls based on your timing rules

**From Copper to EVV (Results):**

1. Caregiver confirms → EVV updated automatically
2. Patient reschedules → EVV updated with new time
3. Can't make it → EVV flagged for your scheduler
4. Voicemail → Logged in EVV with recording

**No manual work required.** The systems stay in sync automatically.

---

### Do I need to give you admin access to my EVV?

**No.** We use secure API connections (read/write access only, not admin).

**What we CAN do:**

- Read schedules and patient/caregiver info
- Update confirmation status
- Add notes and flags

**What we CANNOT do:**

- Delete data
- Change billing/payroll info
- Access financial information
- Modify user permissions

You maintain full control of your EVV system.

---

### What if my EVV system goes down?

**Copper has a built-in backup:**

If we can't reach your EVV:

1. We alert you immediately
2. Calls pause automatically (so we don't make duplicate calls)
3. When EVV comes back online, we resume from where we left off

OR (if you prefer):

- Upload a CSV manually
- We'll make calls from that data
- Sync back to EVV once it's online

---

- Use Copper's provided number (included in all plans)
- Port your existing number to Copper (one-time $50 fee)

### How do call recordings and transcripts work?

### What if someone has a heavy accent or speech impediment?

**Copper is trained on diverse speech patterns,** including:

- Regional accents
- Non-native speakers
- Elderly patients (slower speech, pauses)
- Background noise

**If the AI can't understand:**

1. It politely asks them to repeat
2. After 2-3 attempts, offers to transfer to your office
3. OR takes a callback request

**Accuracy rate:** 92-95% for English, 85-90% for other languages.

---

### Can patients/caregivers interrupt the AI mid-sentence?

**Yes!** Copper uses "interrupt detection."

**How it works:**

- Patient starts speaking mid-sentence
- AI immediately stops talking
- Listens to what they say
- Responds appropriately

**Just like a human conversation.** No waiting for the AI to finish.

---

### What if the call goes to voicemail?

**Copper detects voicemails** and can:

- Leave a pre-recorded message
- Try calling back later (you set the retry rules)
- Send an SMS follow-up (if you have their mobile number)
- Flag for your team to call manually

**You configure the rules:** How many attempts? What times? SMS backup?

## Features & Capabilities

### Can Copper handle complex conversations, or just yes/no questions?

- Takes detailed message
- Flags for manual follow-up

**Accuracy:** 85-90% of calls are handled end-to-end without human intervention.

### Does it update our EVV in real-time or in batches?

**Real-time** (within 30 seconds of call completion).

**Yes!** (Feature in beta, rolling out Q2 2026)

- Caregiver texts YES
- System updates EVV automatically

**Benefits:** Younger caregivers often prefer text. Faster confirmation.

---

### Can I set different calling rules for different scenarios?

**Absolutely.** You control:

**When to call:**

- Shift confirmations: 24 hours before
- Patient reminders: 24 hours before + 2-hour reminder
- Family updates: Immediately after visit

**What times to call:**

- Caregivers: 8am-8pm
- Patients: 9am-6pm
- Families: 10am-7pm

**How many retries:**

- Important calls: 3 attempts over 24 hours
- Routine calls: 1 attempt + SMS

**Custom rules per patient:**

- Mrs. Johnson prefers morning calls
- Mr. Smith is hard of hearing (flag for manual call)
- Family of Patient X wants daily updates

---

### Can I customize the scripts Copper uses?

**Yes!** You have full control over:

- Opening greeting
- Confirmation questions
- Rescheduling options
- Escalation language
- Closing message

**Two options:**

1. **Template-based:** Choose from 20+ pre-built scripts, tweak as needed
2. **Fully custom:** Write your own scripts (we help with best practices)

**Live editing:** Change scripts anytime, takes effect immediately.

---

### What happens if a patient or caregiver is upset/angry?

**Copper is trained to detect emotion** and de-escalate:

**If someone sounds frustrated:**

- AI shifts to empathetic tone
- Offers to transfer to a human immediately
- OR offers to take a detailed message

**Example:**
Patient: "This is the third call today!"
AI: "I'm so sorry for the inconvenience. Let me connect you to our office right away."

**Escalation triggers:**

- Raised voice
- Curse words
- "Let me speak to a person"
- "I want to file a complaint"

**Immediate transfer to your office line.**
