## Cost Breakdown: 1,000 Calls/Month Scenario

**Assumptions:**

- Average call: 3 minutes
- Total minutes: 3,000/month
- Home health agency with 30 caregivers

### Copper AI: $497/month

**What's included:**

- ✅ 1,000 calls/month (up to 2,500 on this tier)
- ✅ Speech-to-text (unlimited)
- ✅ AI LLM processing (unlimited)
- ✅ Text-to-speech (premium voices)
- ✅ Telephony (US numbers included)
- ✅ EVV integration (WellSky, Axxess, ClearCare, Sandata)
- ✅ White-glove setup
- ✅ Ongoing support

**Hidden fees:** $0
**Total: $497/month**

### Vapi AI: ~$540-990/month

**Breakdown:**

- Platform fee: $0 (usage-based)
- Speech-to-text: $0.006/min × 3,000 = $18
- LLM (GPT-4o mini): $0.02/min × 3,000 = $60
- Text-to-speech (ElevenLabs): $0.30/1K chars × ~750K chars = $225
- Telephony (Twilio): $0.014/min × 3,000 = $42

## Objection Handlers

### "Dialora is cheaper. Why pay more for Copper?"

**Answer:**
"Great question. Dialora is cheaper upfront ($197 vs $497), but here's what you miss:

1. **EVV Integration** - With Dialora, you manually enter confirmations into your EVV. With Copper, it syncs automatically. That's 5-10 hours/week saved.

2. **Home health workflows** - Dialora uses generic call scripts. Copper knows your workflows (shift confirmations, HIPAA compliance, state regulations).

3. **True cost** - If you pay someone $25/hr to manually sync Dialora → EVV, that's $500-1,000/month in labor. Copper saves you that cost.

So Dialora looks cheaper, but Copper has better ROI once you factor in the time you save."
