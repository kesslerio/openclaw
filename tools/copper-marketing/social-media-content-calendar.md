### LinkedIn (Primary)

**Target:** Home health agency owners, operations managers, healthcare executives
**Posting frequency:** 3-5x per week

- 40% Educational (industry insights, best practices)

## Engagement Strategy

### Daily Tasks (15-20 minutes):

- [ ] Respond to all comments on your posts (within 2 hours)
- [ ] Comment on 3-5 relevant industry posts
- [ ] Answer 1-2 questions in Facebook groups
- [ ] Like/share content from potential customers

### Weekly Tasks (30-45 minutes):

- [ ] Join 1-2 new relevant conversations
- [ ] Share industry news with your take
- [ ] Send 5-10 connection requests (with personal note)
- [ ] Engage with competitors' content (professionally)

### DON'T:

- ❌ Ghost comments (always respond)
- ❌ Spam groups with promotions
- ❌ Auto-DM people who follow you
- ❌ Buy followers/engagement
- ❌ Post and disappear

## Analytics to Track

### Weekly:

- Post reach/impressions
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Click-through rate (CTR) on links
- Follower growth
- Top-performing posts (what worked?)

### Monthly:

- Website traffic from social
- Demo requests from social
- Leads generated
- Cost per lead (if running ads)
- Engagement trends (up/down/flat)

### Adjust Based On:

- Posts with >5% engagement → Do more of that
- Posts with <1% engagement → Do less of that
- Topics that drive clicks → Create more content
- Best posting times → Schedule around those
