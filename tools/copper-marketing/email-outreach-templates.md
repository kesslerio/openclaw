## Email Outreach Best Practices

### Do's

✅ **Personalize** - Reference their city, EVV system, or specific pain points
✅ **Be specific** - Real numbers, real timelines, real results
✅ **Keep it short** - 100-150 words max
✅ **One CTA** - Don't give too many options
✅ **Value first** - Give something useful (calculator, case study) before asking
✅ **Test subject lines** - A/B test to see what works

### Don'ts

❌ **Generic pitches** - "We help healthcare companies..." (too broad)
❌ **Feature dumps** - Nobody cares about your features, only their problems
❌ **Aggressive follow-ups** - 3-4 emails max, then stop
❌ **Lying about savings** - Only promise what you can prove
❌ **Forgetting mobile** - 50%+ of emails opened on mobile, keep it scannable
