# The Perfect Copper AI Demo Call

**Duration:** 15 minutes  
**Goal:** Show value, build trust, close trial

---

## 🎯 Pre-Call Prep (5 minutes before)

### Research Prospect:

- [ ] LinkedIn: Company size, role, tenure
- [ ] Website: Services offered, locations
- [ ] Google: Recent news, press releases
- [ ] Their EHR (if known): Integration readiness

### Personalize Pitch:

- Calculate THEIR exact ROI (use their bed count)
- Find 1-2 pain points specific to them
- Prep 1 relevant competitor story

### Tech Check:

- [ ] Demo environment loaded
- [ ] Screen share tested
- [ ] Backup: Recorded demo video ready
- [ ] CRM open for note-taking

---

## 📞 The Call Structure

### **Minutes 0-2: Build Rapport**

**You:** "Hi [Name]! Thanks for taking the time. I know you're busy running a [X]-bed agency, so I'll keep this tight and valuable. Sound good?"

**[Wait for agreement]**

**You:** "Quick question before we dive in: What's the biggest headache you have with scheduling right now?"

**[LISTEN. Take notes. Mirror their language later.]**

**Possible answers:**

- "Staff shortage" → Focus on automation
- "After hours calls" → Focus on 24/7
- "No-shows" → Focus on confirmation calls
- "Costs" → Lead with ROI

---

### **Minutes 2-5: Set Context**

**You:** "Perfect. So here's what Copper AI does in one sentence:

We're a HIPAA-compliant voice AI that handles appointment scheduling, confirmations, and rescheduling—24/7, for about $200/month instead of the $2,400 you're probably spending on a human scheduler."

**[Pause for reaction]**

**You:** "Let me show you exactly how it works. I'm going to share my screen and we'll do a live call together. You'll literally hear the AI in action. Ready?"

---

### **Minutes 5-10: Live Demo**

**DEMO FLOW:**

#### 1. Show Dashboard (30 seconds)

- "This is what you see every morning: All calls from yesterday, who was scheduled, who said yes, who needs follow-up."

#### 2. Trigger Live Call (3 minutes)

**You:** "Now watch this. I'm going to call our demo line as if I'm a patient."

**[Make call. Use scenario matching their pain point.]**

**SCENARIO A (If they said "after hours"):**

- Call at "9 PM" (simulated)
- AI answers immediately
- Books appointment
- Confirms availability

**SCENARIO B (If they said "no-shows"):**

- Call as "confirmation 24h before"
- AI reminds patient
- Patient confirms/reschedules
- System updates EHR

**SCENARIO C (If they said "staff shortage"):**

- Show 5 consecutive calls
- AI handles all without human
- "Your scheduler is free to do higher-value work"

#### 3. Show Integration (2 minutes)

**You:** "And here's the magic: This appointment goes directly into [their EHR name]. No double entry, no copy-paste, no errors."

**[Show EHR integration dashboard]**

**You:** "Your nurses just show up. The AI handled the rest."

#### 4. Show Safety Features (1 minute)

**You:** "Now, what if the AI can't handle something? Watch this..."

**[Trigger escalation scenario]**

**You:** "See? Complex cases go straight to your human team. We're not replacing judgment, just the repetitive stuff."

---

### **Minutes 10-12: Address Elephants**

**You:** "Okay, you're probably thinking three things right now:

1. 'Is this HIPAA compliant?'
2. 'Will my elderly patients use it?'
3. 'What if it makes a mistake?'

Let me hit all three..."

#### HIPAA:

"Yes. SOC 2 certified. BAAs with every provider. Audit logs for every call. We handle PHI all day. That's why hospitals trust us."

#### Elderly Patients:

"They dial your existing number. They talk normally. 95% don't realize it's AI. We tested with 500 elderly patients. They prefer it because it's patient, never rushed, and available when they call at 8 PM because they forgot."

#### Mistakes:

"Two safety nets: AI escalates to human if confused. You review all schedules before final confirmation. You're always in control."

---

### **Minutes 12-14: ROI & Trial**

**You:** "Let's talk numbers for [their agency size].

**Current state:**

- You're paying a scheduler $15/hour x 40 hours = $2,400/month
- They handle maybe 50 calls/day
- That's 1,500 calls/month

**With Copper AI:**

- Same 1,500 calls at $0.13 each = $195/month
- **You save: $2,205/month**
- That's $26,460 per year

What would you do with an extra $26k?"

**[Let them answer. They're now picturing the savings.]**

**You:** "Here's what I suggest: We run a 2-week pilot. 50 real calls. You see the results. If it works, we go live. If not, you've lost nothing. Does that sound fair?"

---

### **Minutes 14-15: Close**

**Assuming interest:**

**You:** "Perfect. Here's what happens next:

**Tomorrow:** I'll email you the pilot agreement (1 page, no commitments)  
**Day 2:** Our tech team sets up your integration (we need 2 hours with your IT person)  
**Day 3-4:** We test internally  
**Day 5:** You go live with 10 calls/day for 2 weeks  
**End of Week 2:** We review the data and you decide

Sound good? When's your IT person available for that 2-hour setup?"

**[Get calendar invite scheduled BEFORE hanging up]**

## 🎭 Handling Common Demo Interruptions

### "How much does this cost again?"

**Answer:** "$0.13 per call. For 1,500 calls/month, that's $195. Compare that to your current $2,400/month scheduler. Want me to email you the exact ROI calculation for your agency size?"

### "We tried [Competitor] and it didn't work"

**Answer:** "I hear that a lot. Most AI is generic. We only do home health, so our AI knows your workflows, your terminology, and your patients. That specificity is why it works. Want to see how ours handles [their specific use case]?"

### "Our patients are too old for this"

**Answer:** "Let me show you something..." [Play recording of 80-year-old successfully using it] "...Sound familiar? That's the point. It's a phone call, not an app. They're already comfortable with phones."

### "This seems too good to be true"

**Answer:** "I get that! That's why we offer a pilot. 50 calls, 2 weeks, real patients. You'll see the data yourself. Then you decide. Zero risk. What do you say?"

## 🎯 Demo Success Metrics

**You crushed it if:**

- ✅ They asked about pricing/ROI (interest)
- ✅ They asked about EHR integration (technical buy-in)
- ✅ They mentioned a specific use case (imagining it)
- ✅ They scheduled next step (calendar invite)
- ✅ They asked "When can we start?" (slam dunk!)

**You need recovery if:**

- ❌ "Send me info" with no next step (brush-off)
- ❌ "I'll talk to my team" without timeline (stalling)
- ❌ "Too expensive" without seeing ROI (didn't sell value)

## 💡 Advanced Tips

### 1. Use Their Language

If they say "clients" not "patients" → Mirror that  
If they say "visits" not "appointments" → Use their words

### 2. Name Drop (If True)

"We're working with [Similar Agency] in [Their State]. They had the same concern about elderly patients. Now they're scheduling 2,000 calls/month."

### 3. Handle Multiple Stakeholders

If clinical director says "I need to check with finance":  
**You:** "Perfect! Want me on that call? I can show them the ROI directly. Usually takes 10 minutes to get budget approval when they see the numbers."

### 4. Time Pressure (Ethical)

If they're interested but slow:  
**You:** "We're onboarding 5 new agencies this month. I've got 2 integration slots left in February. If you want to start in March instead of April, we should book now. Want me to hold a slot?"

---

## 🚨 Demo Don'ts

❌ **Don't:** Over-explain the AI technology  
✅ **Do:** Show it working, let them hear it

❌ **Don't:** Bash competitors by name  
✅ **Do:** "Unlike other solutions, we focus only on home health"

❌ **Don't:** Promise customization you can't deliver  
✅ **Do:** "That's a great idea for Phase 2. Let's nail Phase 1 first."

❌ **Don't:** Let them defer without a next step  
✅ **Do:** "Before we hang up, when's good for that IT call?"
