## 🎯 Ideal Customer Profile

**Best-fit agencies:**

- **Size:** 20-200 caregivers
- **Type:** Medicaid-heavy (>50% of revenue)
- **Pain:** High EVV error rate (>10%)
- **Tech:** Low adoption of current EVV app
- **Geography:** States with strict EVV enforcement

**Red flags (not a fit):**

- Medicare-only (EVV not required)
- <10 caregivers (too small)
- Already happy with current EVV system (rare, but exists)
