# EVV Demo Script - Live Sales Call

**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Setup:** Phone + speakerphone OR Loom screen recording  
**Audience:** Home health agency owners/managers  
**Goal:** Show how effortless EVV can be

---

---

### Demo Part 1: Pre-Visit Reminder (1 minute)

**[15 minutes before scheduled visit]**

**YOU:**

**[Pause demo, explain]**

**YOU:**

### Q: "What if the caregiver doesn't answer the call?"

**A:**

> "Great question. The AI tries calling 2-3 times. If no answer, it sends a text message with a callback number. And you get a real-time alert in your dashboard that the visit wasn't verified. This actually gives you _better_ visibility than an app, where the caregiver just forgets and you don't know until later."

---

### Q: "Can they call the AI instead of waiting for the AI to call them?"

**A:**

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Voice Acting Tips:**

1. **AI voice:** Professional, warm, efficient (not robotic)
2. **Caregiver voice:** Natural, slightly rushed (realistic)
3. **Pace:** Slow enough to follow, fast enough to show efficiency
4. **Enthusiasm:** Show genuine excitement when explaining benefits

**Technical Tips:**

1. Use actual phone (more realistic than computer audio)
2. Record a real demo once and reuse it (consistency)
3. Have backup plan if tech fails (just describe it)
4. Keep demo under 5 minutes (attention spans are short)

**Sales Tips:**

1. Pause after each call to explain what happened
2. Point out specific EVV data captured
3. Compare to their current pain points
4. Ask questions throughout (keep them engaged)
5. End with clear next step (pilot proposal)

## 📊 Demo Success Metrics

**Good demo signs:**

- ✅ Prospect says "That's so simple!"
- ✅ They ask about integration (buying signal)
- ✅ They mention specific caregivers who would love this
- ✅ They ask about pricing/pilot
- ✅ They compare to their current frustrations

**Need to improve:**

- ❌ Confused about how it works (demo too fast?)
- ❌ Skeptical about AI accuracy (address head-on)
- ❌ Concerned about cost (show ROI earlier)
- ❌ Silent/disengaged (ask more questions)
