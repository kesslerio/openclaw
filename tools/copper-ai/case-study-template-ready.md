## Use Cases Highlighted

### 1. Pre-Visit Confirmation

**Scenario:** Maria scheduled for 2 PM visit with Mr. Johnson

**How it worked:**

- 1:45 PM: AI calls Maria to confirm
- Maria: "Yes, I'm on my way"
- **Result:** No-show prevented, client satisfaction maintained

### 2. Clock-In Verification

**Scenario:** John arrives at patient's home

**How it worked:**

- John calls EVV hotline
- AI: "Have you arrived at [address]?"
- John: "Yes"
- AI logs: Arrival at 10:03 AM
- **Result:** Perfect EVV data, zero manual entry

### 3. Problem Detection

**Scenario:** Sarah's visit running long

**How it worked:**

- 3-hour visit (scheduled for 2 hours)
- AI: "Is everything okay? This visit is longer than scheduled"
- Sarah: "Patient had a fall, waiting for family"
- AI logs incident, alerts office
- **Result:** Real-time awareness, appropriate response
